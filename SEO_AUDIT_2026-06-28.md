# SEO & Funnel Audit — bestairesumes.com
**2026-06-28 · multi-agent audit (6 analyses + 3 adversarial verifications)**

## Bottom line
Two problems are being conflated:
- **No traffic (indexation):** a self-inflicted technical regression in **late March 2026** demoted the site, and Google now declines to (re)index pages on **quality/trust grounds** — not just crawl-lag.
- **No registrations (funnel):** almost nobody reaches the funnel. The funnel mostly works; two cheap fixes aside, this is downstream of traffic.

## Corrected root cause (git-verified)
NOT the localePrefix migration (that shipped **2026-05-19**, `d1de1597`, *after* the collapse, as a fix attempt).

Actual trigger window **2026-03-27 → 04-04**:
- **`www`→non-`www` redirect loop** (`ERR_TOO_MANY_REDIRECTS`) on the canonical host for ~8 days (fixed `bb0bf5bb` Apr 4).
- Simultaneous **mass sitemap churn** — 8 commits in one day, dumping 24,018 URLs (`43e19953`).
- Googlebot hit broken redirects on a low-authority domain mid content-churn → **rankings slid 9→55 over ~10 days** (a smooth positional slide = ranking demotion, not redirect-orphaning) → impressions evaporated through April.

## Honest prognosis
Re-indexing will NOT restore old numbers:
1. **"30K impressions" is a mirage** — 180-day cumulative from a peak, not run-rate. Real pre-collapse run-rate ≈ **7,000/90d**. Recover target is run-rate.
2. **~20–30% gone by choice** — the 12 noindexed AI locales (good call) include Italy (19.7% of country impressions; top page was an Italian blog post). Keeper ceiling ≈ **5,000–5,500 impr/90d**.
3. **Never a click business** — 43 clicks across the entire 180-day peak; CTR ~0.12%. ~75% of keeper impressions are German/French *informational blog*, not English commercial. English ≈ 25% with almost **zero top-20 footprint**.

**Proof it's quality, not crawl-lag:** flagship English pages (software-engineer, nurse, teacher) were recrawled fresh on Jun 25–26 and **still sit "Crawled – not indexed."** Google saw them and declined.

**Timeline:** clean pages re-index 2–6 wks; English commercial authority = **6–12+ months** of real work.

## 3 things that matter most
1. **Freeze URL architecture/routing.** Three structural changes in 8 weeks each reset Google's clock. Costs nothing.
2. **One source of truth for locale indexability:** `INDEXABLE_LOCALES = ['en','es','fr','de','ar']` — derive robots meta + hreflang + sitemap from it.
3. **It's now a content-quality + authority game.** Templated boilerplate, fake author personas, thin stubs are why even recrawled pages won't index.

## STOP doing
- Re-architecting URLs/routing/locale prefixes.
- Treating 30K as the target (it's ~5K/90d, mostly non-English, non-clicking).
- Generating more templated pages (esp. 1:1 cover-letter mirrors duplicating resume Salary sections).
- Attributing content to real public figures (Ken Coleman, Anna Papalia) with empty profiles — YMYL misattribution + manual-action risk.
- Removing `sitemap-index.xml` from robots.txt (would starve ~5,400 keeper URLs).

## ⚠️ Safety gate before any config change
**Deployed site has DRIFTED from source.** Live: `/pt/` and `/it/` homepages are **indexed** despite being in `NOINDEX_LOCALES` (committed Jun 18). The running build ≠ HEAD. **Redeploy from HEAD and verify live robots + hreflang before touching locale config.**
