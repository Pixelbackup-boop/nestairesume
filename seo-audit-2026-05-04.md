## 3. CRITICAL: Mobile Core Web Vitals

| Page | Mobile Score | LCP (mobile) | Desktop Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homepage | 61/100 | **9.7s** ❌ | 48/100 (also poor) |
| `/en/blog/chatgpt-vs-claude-for-resumes` | 64/100 | 5.7s ❌ | 75/100 |
| `/en/resume-examples/software-engineer` | 59/100 | **9.8s** ❌ | 76/100 |
| `/en/cover-letter-examples/medical-scribe` | 56/100 | 7.4s ❌ | 90/100 |

**LCP target is < 2.5s. Your mobile pages are 2-4x slower.** This is almost certainly your #1 ranking blocker.

### Likely causes (from page inspection)
- Heavy preloaded fonts (10 woff2 files visible in Link header)
- Third-party scripts (Tawk.to chat, Google Analytics, AdSense)
- Possible large hero images without responsive variants

### Why mobile is so much slower than desktop
Same content, but Lighthouse simulates mobile CPU at 4x slowdown + 3G network. Reveals JS execution and asset weight that desktop hides.

### Recommended next steps
1. Profile the homepage with Chrome DevTools mobile throttling
2. Identify what's contributing to LCP (likely a hero image or above-fold component)
3. Lazy-load below-fold content
4. Consider deferring Tawk.to / AdSense until after FCP

**Estimated impact: fixing mobile LCP from ~9s → <2.5s could lift average ranking position by 5-15 places** based on industry data.

## 5. Title duplication bug (FIXED)

Before the fix today, every blog/career-tips/career page rendered:
> `${page-title} | Best AI Resume Blog | Best AI Resume`

That's 95+ characters with "Best AI Resume" appearing twice. Google truncated to ~60 chars, showing users:
> `${page-title} | Best AI Resume Blog | Best AI...`

Looks like a glitch. Likely contributed to the 0.06% CTR on `/blog/chatgpt-vs-claude-for-resumes` (1,663 impressions, 1 click).

**Fix shipped:**
- `app/[locale]/blog/[slug]/page.tsx` — `title: { absolute: ... }`
- `app/[locale]/career-tips/[slug]/page.tsx` — `title: { absolute: ... }`
- `app/[locale]/career/[slug]/page.tsx` — `title: { absolute: ... }`

After this deploys, all blog/career titles will be:
> `${page-title} | Best AI Resume`

(56-70 chars, brand visible once)

## 6. Stale content flag

`/blog/chatgpt-vs-claude-for-resumes` description reads:
> "We tested ChatGPT-4o and Claude 3.5 Sonnet to see which AI..."

Today is May 2026. **GPT-4o was released April 2024 (24+ months old). Claude 3.5 Sonnet was released June 2024 (22+ months old).** Current models: GPT-5, Claude Sonnet 4.6, Claude Opus 4.7.

Users searching "ChatGPT vs Claude resume" in 2026 expect modern model comparisons. The description signals "outdated content" → users skip the result → low CTR despite position 6.

**Action:** Refresh the comparison with current models. Or pivot the URL to `/blog/best-ai-resume-tools-2026` (more evergreen).
