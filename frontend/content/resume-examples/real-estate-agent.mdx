## What Makes a Great Real Estate Agent Resume?

This real estate agent resume example shows exactly what hiring managers want to see. The right resume format makes the difference between landing an interview and getting filtered out. Real estate agents need to demonstrate strong sales performance, client relationship management, and local market knowledge. Brokerages want to see transaction volumes, listing-to-close ratios, and evidence that you can generate your own leads. Your resume should read like a sales performance report, not a generic job description.

## Professional Summary Examples

**For Entry-Level:**
"Licensed Real Estate Agent with 1 year of experience and 8 completed transactions totaling $2.4M in sales volume. Built a client base of 40+ contacts through open houses, social media marketing, and community networking. Proficient in MLS, Zillow Premier Agent, and DocuSign."

**For Mid-Level:**
"Top-performing Real Estate Agent with 5+ years of experience and $18M in career sales volume across 75+ transactions. Consistently ranked in the top 15% of agents at brokerage, maintaining a 92% client satisfaction rate and 35% repeat/referral business. Specializes in residential properties in the greater metropolitan area."

**For Senior:**
"Award-winning Real Estate Agent with 12+ years of experience and $65M+ in career sales volume. Led a team of 4 buyer's agents, closed 30+ transactions annually with an average list-to-sale ratio of 98.5%, and built a referral network generating 50% of annual business. Recognized as Top Producer for 6 consecutive years."

## Real Estate Agent Resume Format & Template Tips

Choosing the right resume format is critical for real estate roles:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for Real Estate Agent candidates with steady career progression
- **Professional resume format** — Use clean section headers, consistent spacing, and a modern but readable font
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Avoid graphics, tables, and columns that confuse applicant tracking systems
- **Resume length** — 1 page for entry-level / 1-2 pages for experienced / 2 pages for senior
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format across all devices and ATS platforms

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not including sales volume or transaction counts** — Numbers are everything in real estate; brokerages hire based on production
2. **Omitting your license information** — Always include your state license number and any designations (CRS, ABR, GRI)
3. **Ignoring digital marketing skills** — Modern real estate demands social media, CRM, and online lead generation expertise
4. **Using vague language** — Replace "helped clients buy homes" with specific transaction counts and dollar amounts
5. **Leaving out specializations** — Luxury, commercial, first-time buyers, or investment properties show your niche expertise
