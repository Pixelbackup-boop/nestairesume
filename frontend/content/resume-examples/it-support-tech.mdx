## What Makes a Great IT Support Technician Resume?

This IT Support Technician resume example shows exactly what hiring managers want to see. The right resume format makes the difference between landing an interview and getting filtered out. IT Support Technicians are the front line of technology support, and your resume must demonstrate quick problem-solving skills, excellent customer service, and the ability to handle high ticket volumes while maintaining quality. Employers look for candidates who can keep employees productive by resolving issues efficiently.

## Professional Summary Examples

**For Entry-Level:**
"IT Support Technician with 6 months of help desk experience and CompTIA A+ certification. Resolved an average of 25 tickets daily with a 94% first-contact resolution rate. Experienced in Windows 10/11 troubleshooting, Office 365 support, and basic network connectivity diagnosis. Known for clear communication with non-technical users."

**For Mid-Level:**
"Reliable IT Support Technician with 3+ years of experience providing Level 1 and Level 2 support for organizations with 300+ users. Handle 40+ tickets daily across hardware, software, and network issues with a 97% customer satisfaction rating. Skilled in Active Directory user management, remote support tools, and printer/peripheral setup across multi-site environments."

**For Senior:**
"Experienced IT Support Technician with 7+ years in enterprise help desk environments, serving as a Level 2/3 escalation point. Mentor a team of 4 junior technicians while personally resolving 30+ escalated tickets per day. Reduced repeat ticket rates by 35% through root cause analysis and proactive documentation. Earned Employee of the Quarter 5 times for exceptional service."

## Essential Skills to Highlight

### Hardware & Desktop Support
- PC, laptop, and Mac hardware troubleshooting
- Hardware imaging and deployment (SCCM, MDT)
- Printer, scanner, and peripheral installation
- Mobile device setup and support (iOS, Android)
- Monitor, docking station, and AV equipment configuration
- Asset tracking and inventory management

### Software & Account Support
- Windows 10/11 and macOS troubleshooting
- Microsoft Office 365 and Google Workspace support
- Software installation, updates, and licensing
- Active Directory account creation and password resets
- VPN and remote access configuration
- Antivirus and endpoint protection management

### Service Delivery
- Ticketing systems (ServiceNow, Zendesk, Freshdesk)
- Phone, email, and chat support channels
- SLA adherence and priority triage
- Remote desktop tools (TeamViewer, AnyDesk, SCCM)
- Knowledge base documentation and FAQ creation
- Customer communication and follow-up

## IT Support Technician Resume Format & Template Tips

Choosing the right resume format is critical for technology support roles:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for IT Support Technician candidates with steady career progression in help desk and support
- **Professional resume format** — Use clean section headers, consistent spacing, and a modern but readable font
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Avoid graphics, tables, and columns that confuse applicant tracking systems
- **Resume length** — 1 page for entry-level / 1-2 pages for experienced / 2 pages for senior
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format across all devices and ATS platforms

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not quantifying ticket volume and resolution speed** — These are the core metrics hiring managers evaluate for support roles
2. **Ignoring customer satisfaction scores** — IT support is a service role, so user feedback scores demonstrate your interpersonal skills
3. **Being too vague about technical skills** — List specific operating systems, tools, and platforms rather than just "computer troubleshooting"
4. **Omitting certifications** — CompTIA A+, ITIL Foundation, and Microsoft certifications set you apart from other candidates
5. **Forgetting to mention multi-channel support** — Specify whether you handled phone, email, chat, or in-person support
