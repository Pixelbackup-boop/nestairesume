## What Makes a Great Cabinet Maker Resume?

A strong cabinet maker resume demonstrates mastery of both traditional woodworking techniques and modern CNC technology. Hiring managers in this trade look for candidates who can read blueprints, operate precision equipment, and deliver flawless custom work that meets architect specifications.

The most effective cabinet maker resumes showcase the types of projects completed—kitchen cabinets, commercial millwork, furniture—and highlight both hand skills and technology proficiency.

## Professional Summary Examples

### Entry-Level Cabinet Maker
Skilled woodworker with vocational training in cabinet construction and wood finishing. Proficient in operating table saws, routers, and edge banders. Strong attention to detail with commitment to quality craftsmanship and continuous skill development.

### Mid-Level Cabinet Maker
Experienced cabinet maker with 4+ years constructing residential and commercial cabinetry. Proficient in CNC programming using Cabinet Vision and operating automated machinery. Known for precision joinery and flawless finish work on high-end custom projects.

### Senior Cabinet Maker
Master cabinet maker with 10+ years crafting custom millwork for luxury residential and commercial clients. Expert in AWI premium grade specifications, complex veneering, and specialty finishes. Mentors apprentices while managing production workflow for the cabinet shop.

## Cabinet Maker Resume Format & Template Tips

Your cabinet maker resume should emphasize both hand skills and technology proficiency:

- **Lead with project types:** Kitchen cabinets, commercial millwork, custom furniture
- **Include software skills:** Cabinet Vision, Mozaik, AutoCAD, eCabinet
- **Specify equipment:** CNC routers, edge banders, wide-belt sanders
- **Highlight finish expertise:** Stains, lacquers, catalyzed finishes
- **Show AWI knowledge:** Premium, custom, and economy grade experience

## Hiring Manager Tip

> **"Modern cabinet shops need people who can do both—traditional hand skills AND CNC programming. If you only know one, you're limiting your value. Show me your CNC software experience alongside your joinery skills, and demonstrate you can read architect specs and produce accurate shop drawings."**

This insight reflects what decision-makers prioritize when reviewing cabinet maker candidates.

## Common Cabinet Maker Interview Questions

### 1. What CNC software and equipment have you used?
List specific software (Cabinet Vision, Mozaik) and machine brands operated.

### 2. How do you ensure accurate measurements on custom work?
Describe your process from field measuring to shop drawing verification.

### 3. What joinery methods do you prefer for different applications?
Discuss pocket screws, dowels, mortise-and-tenon, and when to use each.

### 4. How do you handle wood movement in your designs?
Show understanding of seasonal expansion/contraction and how to accommodate it.

### 5. What finishing systems have you worked with?
Detail experience with conversion varnish, lacquer, and catalyzed finishes.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Ignoring CNC skills** - Modern shops require computer-controlled equipment proficiency
2. **Omitting software experience** - Cabinet design software is essential today
3. **Generic project descriptions** - Specify kitchen, bath, commercial, or furniture focus
4. **Missing finish expertise** - Finishing skills often differentiate candidates
5. **Forgetting AWI standards** - Commercial work requires AWI grade knowledge

## ATS Optimization for Cabinet Maker Resumes

Ensure your resume passes Applicant Tracking Systems by including these keywords naturally:

**Priority Keywords:** cabinet maker, cabinetry, CNC, woodworking

**Additional Keywords:** Cabinet Vision, custom millwork, face frame, frameless, edge banding, wood finishing, shop drawings, AWI

Format your resume as a single-column layout without tables, text boxes, or graphics. Use standard section headings that ATS parsers recognize: Professional Summary, Work Experience, Education, Skills, and Certifications. Save as PDF unless the application specifically requests .docx format. Ensure your contact information appears in the body of the resume, not in headers or footers that some ATS systems skip during parsing.
