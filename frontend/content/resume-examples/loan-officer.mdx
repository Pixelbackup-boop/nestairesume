## Loan Officer Resume Format & Template Tips

Selecting the right resume format is essential for loan officer roles:

- **Chronological resume format** — Best for loan officers with progressive lending experience, showing how your origination volume and portfolio scope have grown over time
- **Professional resume format** — Use a polished layout with clear section headers for Summary, Experience, Skills, Licenses, and Education. Clean formatting reflects the precision expected in financial services
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Avoid complex graphics, tables, or multi-column layouts that applicant tracking systems cannot parse. Use standard headings and clean formatting
- **Resume length** — 1 page for those with under 5 years of experience; 1-2 pages for senior loan officers with extensive production history
- **Lead with volume and compliance metrics** — Place your origination volume, pull-through rates, and compliance records near the top of each role. This resume format ensures lending achievements are immediately visible
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format across all platforms

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **No origination volume** — Lending is a numbers business; always include dollar volume and loan count
2. **Ignoring compliance credentials** — NMLS license and regulatory knowledge are essential; always list them prominently
3. **Missing referral metrics** — Referral networks drive loan officer success; quantify your partner relationships
4. **Generic sales language** — "Generated leads" is vague; "Built 25-agent referral network generating $30M in annual applications" proves impact
5. **Not mentioning loan products** — Specify which products you know (conventional, FHA, VA, jumbo, HELOC) to match job requirements

## ATS Optimization for Loan Officer Resumes

To ensure your resume passes applicant tracking systems, follow this ats resume format guidance:

- Include keywords like "mortgage origination," "credit analysis," "loan processing," "NMLS," "underwriting," and "compliance" naturally throughout your resume
- Use an ats resume template with standard section headings: Professional Summary, Work Experience, Skills, Licenses & Certifications, Education
- Mirror the exact job title and key phrases from the posting — if it says "Mortgage Loan Originator," use that phrase
- Avoid headers-only or graphics-only sections that ATS software cannot read
- Include both spelled-out terms and abbreviations (e.g., "Truth in Lending Act - RESPA Integrated Disclosure (TRID)")
- Place your most important keywords in the top third of your resume where ATS algorithms weight content more heavily
