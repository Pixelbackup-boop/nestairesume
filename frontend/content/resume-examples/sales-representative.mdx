## What Makes a Great Sales Resume?

This sales representative resume example shows exactly what hiring managers want to see. The right resume format makes the difference between landing interviews and getting filtered out. Sales resumes are all about numbers. Hiring managers want to see quota attainment percentages, revenue generated, deal sizes, and growth metrics. If your resume doesn't have numbers, it won't get callbacks.

## Professional Summary Examples

**For Entry-Level (SDR/BDR):**
"Motivated Sales Development Rep with 1 year of experience generating qualified pipeline. Booked 150+ discovery calls in first 6 months, exceeding quota by 120%. Proficient in Salesforce, Outreach, and LinkedIn Sales Navigator."

**For Mid-Level (Account Executive):**
"Results-driven Account Executive with 4+ years in B2B SaaS sales. Consistently exceeded annual quota of $800K, closing $1.2M in ARR last year. Managed full sales cycle from prospecting to enterprise contract negotiation."

**For Senior (Sales Manager):**
"Sales Manager leading team of 10 AEs generating $15M in annual revenue. Grew territory 45% YoY through strategic account planning and team development. Promoted 4 reps to senior roles within 18 months."

## Sales Representative Resume Format & Template Tips

Choosing the right resume format is critical for sales roles:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for sales candidates with steady career progression, listing your most recent quota-carrying experience first
- **Professional resume format** — Use clean section headers, consistent spacing, and a modern but readable font like Calibri or Arial
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Avoid graphics, tables, and columns that confuse applicant tracking systems. Stick to standard section headers
- **Resume length** — 1 page for entry-level SDRs and BDRs, extending to 1-2 pages for experienced account executives and sales managers based on your experience level
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format across all devices and ATS platforms

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **No numbers** — A sales resume without metrics is an instant rejection
2. **Vague quota claims** — Specify the actual dollar amount and attainment %
3. **Listing duties instead of wins** — "Managed accounts" vs. "Grew accounts 40%"
4. **Missing CRM proficiency** — Every sales role requires this
5. **Too long** — One page is ideal; two max for senior roles

## Industry-Specific Tips

### For SaaS/Tech Sales
Emphasize: ARR/MRR, land-and-expand, demo-to-close rate, product knowledge

### For Medical/Pharma Sales
Emphasize: Territory management, physician relationships, regulatory knowledge, clinical training

### For Real Estate
Emphasize: Transaction volume, listing-to-close ratio, market expertise, client retention

### For Financial Services
Emphasize: AUM growth, client acquisition cost, compliance adherence, portfolio performance
