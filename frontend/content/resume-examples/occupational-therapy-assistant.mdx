## What Makes a Great Occupational Therapy Assistant Resume?

COTAs implement occupational therapy treatment plans under OT supervision, helping patients regain independence in daily activities. The role combines hands-on therapy skills with patient motivation and education.

Healthcare employers evaluate Occupational Therapy Assistant candidates on specific credentials, clinical competencies, and demonstrated outcomes. With +23% projected job growth and an average salary of $64,000, competition for top Occupational Therapy Assistant positions requires a resume that immediately communicates your qualifications and value. This guide covers the specific sections, metrics, and formatting that healthcare recruiters look for when reviewing Occupational Therapy Assistant applications.

## Professional Summary Examples

**For Entry-Level Occupational Therapy Assistant:**
"Recently certified Occupational Therapy Assistant with hands-on clinical experience in adl training and treatment implementation. Completed training with focus on documentation and patient-centered care. COTA License certified with strong foundation in healthcare protocols and safety procedures."

**For Experienced Occupational Therapy Assistant:**
"Dedicated Occupational Therapy Assistant with 5+ years of experience in adl training and treatment implementation. Consistently maintained high patient satisfaction scores while managing complex cases. Proficient in adaptive equipment with proven track record of quality outcomes and team collaboration."

**For Senior Occupational Therapy Assistant:**
"Accomplished Occupational Therapy Assistant with 10+ years of progressive experience leading adl training initiatives. Expert in documentation with demonstrated success improving departmental outcomes by 25%. Mentor to junior staff with comprehensive knowledge of exercise programs and regulatory compliance."

## Occupational Therapy Assistant Resume Format & Template Tips

Occupational Therapy Assistant resumes require specific attention to credentials and clinical experience. Here are formatting guidelines tailored to this role:

- **Credentials first** — List your COTA License, NBCOT Certification immediately after your name. Healthcare recruiters verify credentials before reading anything else.
- **Quantify your experience** — Include patient volumes, success rates, and quality metrics. Numbers demonstrate competence better than generic descriptions.
- **Highlight relevant certifications** — Create a dedicated section for certifications with expiration dates. Keep them current and prominently displayed.
- **Use clinical terminology** — Mirror the language from job postings. Include specific equipment, systems, and protocols you're trained on.
- **One page for <5 years experience** — Focus on your most relevant and recent experience. Senior professionals may use two pages for extensive accomplishments.
- **Include continuing education** — Healthcare evolves rapidly. Show your commitment to staying current with recent training and certifications.

## Hiring Manager Tip

 Include your experience with specific patient populations, productivity metrics, and any specialty areas. Mention your comfort level with different supervision models.

## Common Occupational Therapy Assistant Interview Questions

Preparing for interviews is essential for landing your Occupational Therapy Assistant role. Here are questions frequently asked in Occupational Therapy Assistant interviews:

### "How do you handle a patient who wants to change their treatment goals?"

Show understanding of scope—discuss with patient, document, and communicate with supervising OT.

### "Describe your experience with ADL training."

Give specific examples of techniques, adaptive equipment used, and patient outcomes.

### "How do you maintain communication with your supervising OT?"

Discuss documentation, verbal updates, and when to escalate concerns.

### "What therapeutic activities do you find most effective?"

Discuss various activities and how you match them to patient goals and interests.

### "How do you educate families on continuing therapy at home?"

Describe your approach to home programs, demonstrations, and written instructions.

## ATS Optimization for Occupational Therapy Assistant Resumes

Healthcare ATS systems screen for specific credentials, certifications, and clinical keywords. Missing key terms can disqualify an otherwise strong application.

- Include exact credential abbreviations: "COTA License", "NBCOT Certification"
- Use clinical terminology from job postings: "occupational therapy assistant", "COTA", "OTA", "ADL", "rehabilitation"
- List specific equipment, systems, or protocols you're experienced with
- Include both full terms and abbreviations for searchability
- Mention specific patient populations and care settings
