## What Makes a Great Billing Specialist Resume?

This billing specialist resume example shows exactly what hiring managers want to see. The right resume format makes the difference between landing an interview and getting filtered out. Billing specialists must show accuracy, efficiency, and knowledge of billing systems and procedures. Whether you work in medical billing, corporate accounts receivable, or utility billing, your resume should highlight the volume of invoices you process, your collection success rates, and your proficiency with billing software. Employers value candidates who can reduce days sales outstanding and minimize billing errors.

Reduced days sales outstanding from 65 to 38 days, recovering $2.5M in aged receivables over 18 months. Trained 10 billing clerks, implemented automated billing workflows, and managed relationships with 200+ client accounts."

## Essential Skills to Highlight

### Billing & Financial Skills
- **Invoice Processing** — Generating, reviewing, and distributing invoices accurately and on schedule
- **Accounts Receivable** — Managing open balances, applying payments, and aging analysis
- **Collections** — Following up on overdue accounts and negotiating payment arrangements
- **Payment Reconciliation** — Matching payments to invoices and resolving discrepancies
- **Credit Memos & Adjustments** — Processing returns, credits, and billing corrections
- **Month-End Close** — Preparing AR reports, reconciling subledgers, and supporting financial close

### Industry-Specific Skills
- **Medical Billing** — CPT, ICD-10, and HCPCS coding for insurance claim submission
- **Insurance Verification** — Confirming patient eligibility and benefits prior to service
- **Denial Management** — Analyzing and appealing denied claims to maximize reimbursement
- **Contract Billing** — Managing milestone-based or recurring billing per contract terms
- **Regulatory Compliance** — HIPAA for healthcare billing, SOX for corporate environments
- **Revenue Recognition** — Understanding ASC 606 standards for proper revenue recording

### Software & Soft Skills
- **ERP Systems** — SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, and Microsoft Dynamics for enterprise billing
- **Medical Billing Software** — Epic, Athenahealth, Kareo, and AdvancedMD
- **Microsoft Excel** — Pivot tables, VLOOKUP, and macros for billing analysis
- **Attention to Detail** — Catching errors before invoices are sent to prevent delays and disputes
- **Communication** — Coordinating with clients, sales teams, and management on billing issues
- **Organization** — Managing high volumes of invoices and maintaining orderly records

## Billing Specialist Resume Format & Template Tips

Choosing the right resume format is critical for finance roles:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for billing specialist candidates with steady career progression, listing your most recent billing experience first
- **Professional resume format** — Use clean section headers, consistent spacing, and a modern but readable font like Calibri or Arial
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Avoid graphics, tables, and columns that confuse applicant tracking systems. Stick to standard section headers
- **Resume length** — 1 page for entry-level / 1-2 pages for experienced / 2 pages for senior based on your experience level
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format across all devices and ATS platforms

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not quantifying volume and accuracy** — Always state how many invoices you processed and your accuracy rate
2. **Omitting billing software experience** — Name the specific ERP or billing systems you have used
3. **Forgetting coding certifications** — CPC, CCS, or CBCS certifications are highly valued in medical billing and should be listed
4. **Using generic financial language** — Be specific about whether you handle medical billing, corporate AR, or subscription billing
5. **Ignoring collection results** — DSO reduction and recovery amounts show direct financial impact
