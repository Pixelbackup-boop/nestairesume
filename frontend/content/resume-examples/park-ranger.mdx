## What Makes a Great Park Ranger Resume?

Park rangers protect natural resources, educate visitors, and ensure park safety. With +5% growth and $45,000 average salary, Park Ranger positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated Park Ranger with training in Natural Resource Management and Visitor Services. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Park Ranger with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Natural Resource Management and Law Enforcement with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

Park Ranger professionals earn approximately **$45,000** with **+5%** projected growth.
