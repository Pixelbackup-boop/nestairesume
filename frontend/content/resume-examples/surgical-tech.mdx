## What Makes a Great Surgical Technologist Resume?

This surgical technologist resume example shows exactly what hiring managers want to see. The right resume format makes the difference between landing an interview and getting filtered out. Surgical technologists play a critical role in the operating room, and your resume must emphasize precision, sterile technique mastery, and the ability to anticipate surgeon needs. Include your certification status (CST), the types and volume of procedures you have assisted with, and any specialized surgical experience. Healthcare employers value attention to detail and a proven commitment to patient safety.

## Professional Summary Examples

**For Entry-Level:**
"Certified Surgical Technologist (CST) and recent graduate of an accredited surgical technology program with 400+ clinical hours across general surgery, orthopedics, and OB/GYN. Assisted in 120+ procedures during clinical rotations. BLS certified with strong knowledge of sterile technique and surgical instrumentation."

**For Mid-Level:**
"Certified Surgical Technologist with 4 years of operating room experience across general, orthopedic, and cardiovascular surgery. Scrubbed on 2,000+ procedures with a zero surgical site infection rate attributable to breaks in sterile technique. Skilled in robotic surgery setup (da Vinci) and complex instrument management."

**For Senior:**
"Senior Certified Surgical Technologist with 10+ years of experience in Level I trauma center operating rooms. Specialized in neurosurgery and cardiovascular procedures, scrubbing on 5,000+ cases. Precepted 20+ surgical technology students and developed an instrument standardization protocol that reduced setup time by 15%."

## Essential Skills to Highlight

### Clinical & Surgical Skills
- Sterile field creation and maintenance
- Surgical instrument preparation and passing
- Patient positioning and draping
- Wound closure and dressing application
- Specimen collection and handling
- Counts verification (sponges, sharps, instruments)

### Specialized Procedures
- General surgery assistance
- Orthopedic procedures (joint replacement, fracture repair)
- Cardiovascular and thoracic surgery
- Robotic surgery setup (da Vinci system)
- Neurosurgery and spinal procedures
- Laparoscopic and minimally invasive surgery

### Professional Competencies
- Surgeon preference card management
- Operating room turnover efficiency
- Emergency and trauma case readiness
- Infection control and prevention
- HIPAA compliance and patient confidentiality
- Electronic health record (EHR) documentation

## Achievement-Focused Bullet Points

- "Scrubbed on 2,500+ surgical procedures across general, orthopedic, and cardiovascular specialties with a zero attributable surgical site infection rate"
- "Reduced operating room turnover time by 12 minutes on average by implementing a standardized instrument setup protocol for the 5 most common procedures"
- "Maintained 100% accuracy on surgical counts across 800+ consecutive cases, contributing to the department's safety excellence recognition"
- "Trained and precepted 15 surgical technology students during clinical rotations, with all students passing their certification exam on the first attempt"
- "Mastered da Vinci robotic surgery setup and troubleshooting, becoming the go-to scrub tech for 95% of robotic procedures in the facility"
- "Developed updated surgeon preference cards for 8 surgeons, reducing instrument request errors by 40% and improving case preparation efficiency"

## Surgical Technologist Resume Format & Template Tips

Choosing the right resume format is critical for healthcare roles:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for surgical technologist candidates with steady career progression and increasing specialization
- **Professional resume format** — Use clean section headers, consistent spacing, and a modern but readable font
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Avoid graphics, tables, and columns that confuse applicant tracking systems
- **Resume length** — 1 page for entry-level / 1-2 pages for experienced / 2 pages for senior
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format across all devices and ATS platforms

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not listing CST certification prominently** — Your Certified Surgical Technologist credential should be visible in your header or summary
2. **Being vague about procedure volume** — Hiring managers want to know how many and what types of cases you have scrubbed
3. **Omitting specialty experience** — If you have experience in orthopedics, cardiac, neuro, or robotics, feature it prominently
4. **Ignoring clinical hours for new graduates** — Include the number of clinical hours and procedures assisted during your training
5. **Forgetting continuing education** — List relevant CE credits, BLS/ACLS certifications, and any advanced training completed
