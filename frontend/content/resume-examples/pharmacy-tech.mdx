## What Makes a Great Pharmacy Tech Resume?

This pharmacy tech resume example shows exactly what hiring managers want to see. The right resume format makes the difference between landing an interview and getting filtered out. Pharmacy technicians carry significant responsibility in medication preparation and dispensing, so your resume must emphasize certification, accuracy, and your ability to work under the supervision of licensed pharmacists. Highlight your CPhT status, daily prescription volume, and any specialized experience in compounding or IV preparation.

## Professional Summary Examples

**For Entry-Level:**
"CPhT-certified pharmacy technician with 1 year of retail pharmacy experience processing 250+ prescriptions daily. Maintained 99.9% dispensing accuracy while managing prescription intake, insurance adjudication, and patient consultations. Trained in sterile compounding and USP 797 standards."

**For Mid-Level:**
"Experienced pharmacy technician with 5 years in hospital and retail settings, processing 350+ prescriptions daily with 99.95% accuracy. Certified in IV admixture and sterile compounding with expertise in automated dispensing systems (Pyxis, Omnicell). Mentored 10 new technicians through onboarding and certification preparation."

**For Senior:**
"Lead pharmacy technician with 9+ years of experience and CPhT-Advanced certification. Supervised daily operations of a high-volume pharmacy filling 600+ prescriptions per day with a team of 6 technicians. Implemented barcode scanning verification system that reduced dispensing errors by 45% and passed all Board of Pharmacy inspections."

## Essential Skills to Highlight

### Medication Dispensing
- Prescription interpretation and data entry
- Medication counting, pouring, and labeling
- Automated dispensing systems (Pyxis, Omnicell)
- Controlled substance inventory and DEA compliance
- Medication therapy management support
- Prior authorization processing

### Compounding & Preparation
- Non-sterile compounding techniques
- IV admixture and sterile compounding (USP 797/800)
- Unit-dose packaging and repackaging
- Hazardous drug handling procedures
- Vaccine preparation and immunization support
- Total parenteral nutrition (TPN) preparation

### Pharmacy Systems & Compliance
- Epic Willow, Cerner, QS/1, RX30
- Insurance claim adjudication and troubleshooting
- HIPAA and patient privacy compliance
- State and federal pharmacy regulations
- Inventory management and wholesaler ordering
- Quality assurance and error reporting

## Pharmacy Tech Resume Format & Template Tips

Choosing the right resume format is critical for healthcare roles:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for pharmacy tech candidates with steady career progression
- **Professional resume format** — Use clean section headers, consistent spacing, and a modern but readable font
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Avoid graphics, tables, and columns that confuse applicant tracking systems
- **Resume length** — 1 page for entry-level / 1-2 pages for experienced / 2 pages for senior
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format across all devices and ATS platforms

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not listing CPhT certification prominently** — This is the most important credential for pharmacy techs and should be at the top
2. **Omitting prescription volume** — Daily prescription count is the primary measure of your workload capacity
3. **Ignoring compounding experience** — Sterile and non-sterile compounding skills command higher pay and more opportunities
4. **Forgetting state licensure details** — Include your state pharmacy technician license number and expiration
5. **Not differentiating from pharmacy assistants** — Emphasize your dispensing authority, compounding training, and certification level
