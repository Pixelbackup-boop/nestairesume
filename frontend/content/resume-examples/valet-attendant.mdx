## What Makes a Great Valet Attendant Resume?

Valet attendants create first impressions while safely handling guests' vehicles. Speed, care, and professionalism are essential. With +6% growth and $30,000 average salary, Valet Attendant roles offer solid hospitality careers.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Enthusiastic Valet Attendant with training in Driving Skills and Customer Service. Strong commitment to guest satisfaction and team success."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Valet Attendant with 4+ years in hospitality. Proven record in Driving Skills and Vehicle Care with excellent guest feedback."

## Salary & Outlook

Valet Attendant professionals earn approximately **$30,000** with **+6%** growth projected.

**Sources:** [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/transportation-and-material-moving/parking-attendants.htm), [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/), [PayScale](https://www.payscale.com/).

## Essential Skills

### Driving
- Manual transmission
- Luxury vehicles
- SUVs/trucks
- Safe parking

### Service
- Guest greeting
- Vehicle retrieval
- Key management
- Tip handling

### Operations
- Lot organization
- Traffic management
- Vehicle inspection
- Damage documentation



## Common Mistakes

### Not highlighting clean driving record

No accidents/tickets is essential for valet positions

### Missing vehicle experience

Luxury, manual transmission, exotics—show vehicle comfort



## Hiring Manager Tip

> **Valet Attendant resumes with guest satisfaction metrics and operational achievements get prioritized.**

"Valet attendant at luxury hotel parking 100+ vehicles nightly, experienced with exotic and luxury vehicles. Perfect safety record with no damage claims." Show volume, vehicle types, and safety record.

## Interview Questions

### "Can you drive manual transmission?"

Be honest—luxury venues often have manual vehicles.

### "How do you handle a busy arrival rush?"

Discuss prioritization while maintaining care and service.
