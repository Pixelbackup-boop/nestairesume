## What Makes a Great Bank Teller Resume?

This bank teller resume example shows what branch managers and banking recruiters look for in top candidates. The right resume format emphasizes your accuracy with financial transactions, customer service excellence, and ability to identify cross-selling opportunities. A strong bank teller resume example moves beyond listing basic teller duties — it quantifies transaction volumes, balancing accuracy, referral numbers, and customer satisfaction scores. Employers want to see your experience with banking software, compliance knowledge, and sales contribution. Your resume format should present your numerical precision and interpersonal skills in a way that signals reliability and trustworthiness. Banks value consistency and attention to detail, and your resume should reflect both qualities.

## Bank Teller Resume Format & Template Tips

The right resume format tells branch managers you're accurate, trustworthy, and customer-focused:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for bank tellers showing consistent financial industry experience; lead with your most recent branch role highlighting daily transaction volume and accuracy record
- **Resume format for career changers** — Emphasize cash handling, customer service, and compliance experience from retail, hospitality, or administrative roles using this resume format to frame transferable skills
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Include banking software names, compliance certifications, transaction volumes, and product referral metrics. Major banks and credit unions use ATS platforms for all hiring
- **Resume length** — Keep to 1 page; use a concise resume format that prioritizes accuracy metrics, sales referrals, and customer satisfaction scores
- **Compliance section** — Add a certifications section for BSA/AML training, notary public credentials, and any banking licenses within your resume format hierarchy

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **No transaction volume** — Daily transaction count and cash drawer amount are the first metrics branch managers evaluate
2. **Missing accuracy record** — Balancing accuracy and discrepancy-free streaks prove your reliability with financial transactions
3. **Ignoring sales contributions** — Cross-selling referrals and product recommendations demonstrate your value beyond basic transactions
4. **Vague compliance language** — Name specific regulations (BSA, AML, CIP) and training certifications rather than generic "followed procedures"
5. **Overlooking technology** — Banking software proficiency varies between institutions; list the exact platforms you've used

## ATS Optimization for Bank Teller Resumes

Banks and credit unions of all sizes use applicant tracking systems to screen teller candidates. Your ats resume format must include exact terms from the posting: "cash handling," "transaction processing," "customer service," "cross-selling," and "compliance." Use an ats resume template that places your accuracy metrics, banking software, and certifications in clearly labeled sections. Spell out regulatory abbreviations — write "Bank Secrecy Act (BSA)" and "Anti-Money Laundering (AML)" in full at first use. A clean, single-column layout with standard section headers ensures your resume passes ATS screening at major financial institutions.
