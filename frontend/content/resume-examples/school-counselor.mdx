## What Makes a Great School Counselor Resume?

School counselors support students' academic, career, and social-emotional development. With +10% growth and $60,000 average salary, School Counselor positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated School Counselor with training in Counseling and Academic Planning. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled School Counselor with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Counseling and Crisis Intervention with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

School Counselor professionals earn approximately **$60,000** with **+10%** projected growth.
