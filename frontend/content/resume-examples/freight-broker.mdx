## What Makes a Great Freight Broker Resume?

A strong freight broker resume demonstrates your ability to deliver results in the transportation field. Hiring managers in this industry specifically look for candidates who can show measurable impact in negotiation and carrier relations.

Transportation hiring managers verify certifications, safety records, and route experience. Your resume should prominently feature your license class, endorsements, safety record, and specific experience with vehicle types or route complexities relevant to the position.

The most effective freight broker resumes go beyond listing job duties. They showcase quantifiable achievements that prove you can excel in this role.

## Freight Broker Resume Format & Template Tips

Your freight broker resume should emphasize relevant experience prominently. Consider these formatting recommendations:

- **Lead with impact:** Open with a summary highlighting your strongest transportation achievements
- **Quantify results:** Include specific metrics like percentages, dollar amounts, or volume handled
- **Highlight certifications:** Feature credentials like Freight Broker License prominently
- **Use industry keywords:** Include terms like "freight broker", "load matching", "carrier relations" for ATS optimization
- **Keep it concise:** One page for entry-level, two pages maximum for experienced professionals

## Hiring Manager Tip

> **"Freight brokers are measured by revenue and margin. Include loads moved per month, average margin percentage, and key accounts developed."**

This insight reflects what decision-makers prioritize when reviewing freight broker candidates. Make sure your resume addresses these specific concerns directly.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Focusing on duties instead of achievements** - Describe what you accomplished, not just what you were responsible for
2. **Missing relevant keywords** - Include industry terms like freight broker, load matching, carrier relations for ATS screening
3. **Omitting certifications** - Credentials like Freight Broker License can set you apart
4. **Using generic descriptions** - Be specific about your contributions and their impact
5. **Neglecting soft skills** - Transportation roles require strong interpersonal abilities alongside technical skills

## ATS Optimization for Freight Broker Resumes

Ensure your resume passes Applicant Tracking Systems by including these keywords naturally throughout your document:

**Priority Keywords:** freight broker, load matching, carrier relations, TMS

**Additional Keywords:** load board, rate negotiation, logistics sales

Format your resume as a single-column layout without tables, text boxes, or graphics. Use standard section headings that ATS parsers recognize: Professional Summary, Work Experience, Education, Skills, and Certifications. Save as PDF unless the application specifically requests .docx format. Ensure your contact information appears in the body of the resume, not in headers or footers that some ATS systems skip during parsing.

Match the exact phrasing from job descriptions when possible, but maintain natural readability.
