## What Makes a Great Shipping & Receiving Clerk Resume?

This shipping & receiving clerk resume example shows exactly what hiring managers want to see. The right resume format makes the difference between landing an interview and getting filtered out. Shipping and receiving clerks are essential to supply chain operations, handling the physical flow of goods in and out of warehouses and distribution centers. Your resume should emphasize accuracy in inventory management, familiarity with shipping software and warehouse management systems, and physical capability to handle the demands of a warehouse environment. Speed, precision, and safety are the three pillars employers evaluate.

## Professional Summary Examples

**For Entry-Level:**
"Dependable Shipping & Receiving Clerk with 8 months of warehouse experience processing 100+ inbound and outbound shipments daily. Maintained 99% order accuracy using RF scanners and WMS software, operated sit-down forklifts safely, and earned OSHA 10-hour certification. Available for all shifts including overnight."

**For Mid-Level:**
"Efficient Shipping & Receiving Clerk with 3+ years of experience in a high-volume distribution center processing 500+ daily orders. Reduced shipping errors by 25% through implementation of barcode verification protocols, managed inventory for 5,000+ SKUs, and trained 10 new warehouse associates on receiving procedures."

**For Senior:**
"Experienced Shipping & Receiving Lead with 7+ years in logistics and distribution management. Supervised a team of 8 clerks processing $2M+ in daily shipments, improved dock-to-stock time by 30%, and maintained a 99.8% inventory accuracy rate across a 100,000 sq ft warehouse. Zero safety incidents over 3 consecutive years."

## Essential Skills to Highlight

### Shipping & Receiving Operations
- Inbound Receiving & Inspection
- Outbound Order Picking & Packing
- Freight Loading & Unloading
- Bill of Lading & Packing Slip Processing

### Warehouse & Inventory
- Warehouse Management Systems (WMS)
- RF Scanner & Barcode Technology
- Inventory Counting & Cycle Counts
- FIFO & LIFO Inventory Methods
- Putaway & Bin Location Management
- SKU Management & Organization

### Equipment & Safety
- Forklift Operation (Sit-Down, Stand-Up, Reach)
- Pallet Jack & Hand Truck Operation
- OSHA Safety Compliance
- Hazardous Material Handling (HAZMAT)
- PPE Usage & Workplace Safety
- Dock Door & Loading Bay Management

## Shipping & Receiving Clerk Resume Format & Template Tips

Choosing the right resume format is critical for logistics roles:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for Shipping & Receiving Clerk candidates with steady career progression
- **Professional resume format** — Use clean section headers, consistent spacing, and a modern but readable font
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Avoid graphics, tables, and columns that confuse applicant tracking systems
- **Resume length** — 1 page for entry-level / 1-2 pages for experienced / 2 pages for senior
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format across all devices and ATS platforms

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not including volume metrics** — Shipments per day, SKU counts, and warehouse square footage show your operational capacity
2. **Omitting certifications** — Forklift certification, OSHA 10/30, and HAZMAT handling credentials are often required
3. **Ignoring accuracy rates** — Inventory accuracy and order accuracy percentages are the top KPIs for this role
4. **Being vague about technology** — Name specific WMS platforms, RF scanners, and shipping software you have used
5. **Leaving out safety records** — A clean safety record is a significant asset in warehouse environments and should be highlighted
