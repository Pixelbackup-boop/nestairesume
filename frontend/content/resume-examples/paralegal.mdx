## What Makes a Great Paralegal Resume?

Paralegals support attorneys with research, documentation, and case management. The role requires attention to detail and understanding of legal procedures. With +4% growth and $58,000 average salary, Paralegal positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated Paralegal with training in Legal Research and Document Drafting. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Paralegal with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Legal Research and Case Management with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

Paralegal professionals earn approximately **$58,000** with **+4%** projected growth.
