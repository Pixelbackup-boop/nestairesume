## What Makes a Great Computer Technician Resume?

This computer technician resume example shows exactly what hiring managers want to see. The right resume format makes the difference between landing an interview and getting filtered out. A strong computer technician resume demonstrates hands-on expertise in diagnosing and resolving hardware and software issues efficiently. Employers want technicians who can minimize downtime, communicate clearly with non-technical users, and maintain high customer satisfaction while handling a busy ticket queue.

## Professional Summary Examples

**For Entry-Level:**
"CompTIA A+ certified computer technician with 1 year of experience providing desktop support for a 150-user office environment. Resolved an average of 25 tickets per day with a 94% first-contact resolution rate. Proficient in Windows 10/11, macOS, Active Directory, and basic network troubleshooting."

**For Mid-Level:**
"Computer Technician with 5 years of experience supporting 500+ end users across multiple office locations. Maintained a 97% customer satisfaction rating while resolving 40+ daily support requests spanning hardware repairs, software deployments, and network connectivity issues. Led a PC refresh project migrating 350 workstations to Windows 11 ahead of schedule."

**For Senior:**
"Senior Computer Technician with 10+ years of experience managing IT infrastructure for mid-size organizations. Oversee a team of 3 technicians supporting 800 endpoints across 4 locations. Reduced average ticket resolution time by 35% through knowledge base development and remote support tool implementation. Manage annual hardware procurement budgets of $250,000."

## Essential Skills to Highlight

### Hardware & Systems
- Desktop and laptop repair (Dell, HP, Lenovo)
- Component-level troubleshooting (RAM, SSD, GPU, PSU)
- Printer and peripheral device setup
- Server hardware maintenance
- Mobile device support (iOS, Android)
- Imaging and deployment (SCCM, MDT)

### Software & Networking
- Windows 10/11 and macOS administration
- Active Directory and Group Policy
- Microsoft 365 administration
- TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP configuration
- VPN and remote desktop support
- Antivirus and endpoint security

### Support & Documentation
- Help desk ticketing systems (ServiceNow, Zendesk, Jira)
- ITIL service management practices
- Knowledge base documentation
- Asset inventory management
- Customer communication and training
- Escalation procedures and SLA management

## Computer Technician Resume Format & Template Tips

Choosing the right resume format is critical for technology roles:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for computer technician candidates with steady career progression
- **Professional resume format** — Use clean section headers, consistent spacing, and a modern but readable font
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Avoid graphics, tables, and columns that confuse applicant tracking systems
- **Resume length** — 1 page for entry-level / 1-2 pages for experienced / 2 pages for senior
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format across all devices and ATS platforms

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not listing certifications prominently** — CompTIA A+, Network+, and Microsoft certifications are frequently required; place them near the top
2. **Omitting ticket volume and resolution metrics** — These numbers are the primary way employers evaluate technician productivity
3. **Ignoring customer satisfaction scores** — Technical skills alone are not enough; demonstrate your communication abilities
4. **Being vague about environment size** — Always state the number of users, endpoints, and locations you supported
5. **Forgetting to mention specific tools** — Ticketing systems, imaging tools, and remote support platforms should be listed by name
