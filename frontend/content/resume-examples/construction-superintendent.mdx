## What Makes a Great Construction Superintendent Resume?

This construction superintendent resume example shows exactly what hiring managers want to see. The right resume format makes the difference between landing an interview and getting filtered out. A strong construction superintendent resume proves you can manage field operations from ground-breaking to closeout, keeping projects on schedule, within budget, and up to safety and quality standards. General contractors and developers want superintendents who lead by example and manage complex stakeholder relationships.

## Professional Summary Examples

**For Entry-Level:**
"Assistant superintendent with 3 years of field experience on commercial construction projects valued up to $8M. Coordinated daily activities for 6 subcontractor crews and maintained site safety logs with zero recordable incidents over 200,000 man-hours. OSHA 30-Hour certified with proficiency in Procore and Bluebeam."

**For Mid-Level:**
"Construction Superintendent with 7 years of experience managing ground-up commercial and multifamily projects ranging from $5M to $35M. Delivered 12 projects with an average schedule variance of -3 days (ahead of schedule) and maintained a safety record of 0.8 TRIR. Skilled in subcontractor management, owner relations, and change order negotiation."

**For Senior:**
"Senior Construction Superintendent with 15+ years of experience overseeing projects totaling $180M in combined value, including healthcare facilities, mixed-use developments, and K-12 schools. Managed field teams of up to 150 tradespeople and 25 subcontractors simultaneously. Track record of completing 90% of projects ahead of schedule with zero OSHA violations."

## Construction Superintendent Resume Format & Template Tips

Choosing the right resume format is critical for construction roles:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for construction superintendent candidates with steady career progression
- **Professional resume format** — Use clean section headers, consistent spacing, and a modern but readable font
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Avoid graphics, tables, and columns that confuse applicant tracking systems
- **Resume length** — 1 page for entry-level / 1-2 pages for experienced / 2 pages for senior
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format across all devices and ATS platforms

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not listing project values and types** — Employers assess your experience level by the size and complexity of projects you have managed
2. **Omitting safety record** — TRIR, EMR, and OSHA compliance are critical hiring criteria for superintendent roles
3. **Ignoring schedule performance** — On-time delivery is the top performance metric; always include schedule outcomes
4. **Being vague about team size** — Quantify the number of subcontractors, tradespeople, and crew members you managed
5. **Forgetting technology proficiency** — Procore, PlanGrid, Primavera, and Bluebeam are expected tools; list them explicitly
