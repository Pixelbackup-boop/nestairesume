## What Makes a Great Librarian Resume?

Librarians manage library resources and help patrons with research and information needs. With +6% growth and $62,000 average salary, Librarian positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated Librarian with training in Collection Management and Research Assistance. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Librarian with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Collection Management and Information Literacy with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

Librarian professionals earn approximately **$62,000** with **+6%** projected growth.

**Sources:** [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/education-training-and-library/librarians-and-library-media-specialists.htm), [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/), [PayScale](https://www.payscale.com/).

## Resume Tips

- Lead with relevant certifications and qualifications
- Quantify achievements with specific metrics
- Highlight skills matching the job description
- Keep formatting clean and professional
- Include relevant keywords for ATS systems
