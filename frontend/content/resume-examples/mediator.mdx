## What Makes a Great Mediator Resume?

Mediators help parties reach agreements in disputes. Communication skills and neutrality are essential. With +6% growth and $65,000 average salary, Mediator positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated Mediator with training in Conflict Resolution and Communication. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Mediator with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Conflict Resolution and Negotiation with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

Mediator professionals earn approximately **$65,000** with **+6%** projected growth.
