## What Makes a Great Account Executive Resume?

An outstanding account executive resume example tells a story of revenue generation, relationship building, and consistent quota attainment. The ideal resume format for account executive roles puts numbers at the forefront — closed deals, pipeline value, revenue growth, and win rates. This resume example proves to sales leaders that you are a top performer who can hunt new business, manage complex sales cycles, and retain key accounts. An effective resume format for sales positions leads with results, not responsibilities, because every sales manager wants to see your track record before anything else.

Generated $15M+ in career revenue with a 35% average win rate on competitive deals. Led strategic account planning for Fortune 500 clients including 3 seven-figure expansions. President's Club winner 4 consecutive years. Expert in MEDDPICC, Challenger Sale, and executive-level presentations."

## Account Executive Resume Format & Template Tips

The right resume format positions your sales career for the next level:

- **Chronological resume format** — Best for account executives with progressive sales experience, clearly showing increasing quota responsibility, deal sizes, and strategic account ownership
- **Results-first resume format** — Consider adding a "Sales Performance" section at the top of your resume that lists quota attainment, total revenue closed, and awards before your detailed work history using this resume format approach
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Sales organizations use ATS software to screen candidates; use clean formatting with standard headers and ensure your revenue numbers and quota percentages are in plain text, not embedded in graphics
- **Resume length** — 1 page for AEs with under 5 years of experience; 2 pages is acceptable for senior AEs with complex enterprise deal histories
- Always include a clear metrics summary in your resume format — quota, attainment percentage, deal size range, and portfolio size

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not leading with numbers** — An AE resume without revenue figures, quota attainment, and deal sizes will be immediately passed over by sales leadership
2. **Being vague about deal complexity** — Specify whether you sold to SMB, mid-market, or enterprise; include average deal size and sales cycle length
3. **Omitting sales methodology** — MEDDPICC, Challenger, SPIN, and other frameworks show you have a structured approach to selling
4. **Forgetting CRM proficiency** — Salesforce, HubSpot, and Gong are table stakes; not mentioning them raises red flags about your tech fluency
5. **Listing responsibilities instead of results** — "Managed accounts" means nothing; "Grew account portfolio revenue by 35% YoY to $2.1M" tells the winning story

## ATS Optimization for Account Executive Resumes

Sales organizations use applicant tracking systems to filter candidates based on revenue performance and industry experience. Build your resume with an ats resume format that mirrors the language from the job posting. Include keywords like "new business development," "pipeline management," "contract negotiation," "CRM systems," "quota achievement," "strategic selling," "client retention," and "presentation skills" throughout your experience section. Use an ats resume template with single-column formatting, standard section headers, and no tables, graphics, or text boxes. Spell out abbreviations on first use (e.g., "Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)") so the ATS captures every relevant qualification.
