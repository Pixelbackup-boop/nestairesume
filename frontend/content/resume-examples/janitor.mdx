## What Makes a Great Janitor Resume?

Janitors maintain cleanliness and order in buildings through daily cleaning tasks. With +6% growth and $32,000 average salary, Janitor positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated Janitor with training in Cleaning and Floor Care. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Janitor with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Cleaning and Equipment Operation with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

Janitor professionals earn approximately **$32,000** with **+6%** projected growth.

**Sources:** [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/building-and-grounds-cleaning/janitors-and-building-cleaners.htm), [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/), [PayScale](https://www.payscale.com/).
