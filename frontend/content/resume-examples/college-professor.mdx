## What Makes a Great College Professor Resume?

College professors teach undergraduate and graduate students while conducting research in their fields. With +12% growth and $82,000 average salary, College Professor positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated College Professor with training in Subject Expertise and Research. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled College Professor with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Subject Expertise and Curriculum Development with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

College Professor professionals earn approximately **$82,000** with **+12%** projected growth.

**Sources:** [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/education-training-and-library/postsecondary-teachers.htm), [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/), [PayScale](https://www.payscale.com/).

## Key Skills to Include

- Subject Expertise
- Research
- Curriculum Development
- Lecturing
- Student Advising
- Publication
- Grant Writing
- Academic Service

## Resume Tips

- Lead with relevant certifications and qualifications
- Quantify achievements with specific metrics
- Highlight skills matching the job description
- Keep formatting clean and professional
- Include relevant keywords for ATS systems
