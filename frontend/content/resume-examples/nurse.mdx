## What Makes a Great Nursing Resume?

This nurse resume example shows exactly what hiring managers want to see. The right resume format makes the difference between landing an interview and getting filtered out. A successful nursing resume balances clinical expertise with compassionate patient care. Healthcare recruiters look for specific certifications, measurable patient outcomes, and evidence of continuous professional development.

## Professional Summary Examples

**For New Graduate RN:**
"Compassionate BSN graduate with clinical rotations in med-surg, pediatrics, and ICU settings. Achieved 98% patient satisfaction scores during clinicals. BLS and ACLS certified with strong EHR proficiency."

**For Experienced RN:**
"Dedicated Registered Nurse with 5+ years in fast-paced emergency department settings. Managed care for 20+ patients per shift while maintaining 95% patient satisfaction. Preceptor for 10+ new graduate nurses."

**For Specialized Nurse:**
"ICU Registered Nurse with 8+ years of critical care experience and CCRN certification. Reduced ventilator-associated pneumonia rates by 30% through protocol implementation. Expert in hemodynamic monitoring and mechanical ventilation."

## Essential Certifications

### Required
- Current RN License (include state and license number)
- Basic Life Support (BLS)

### Highly Valuable
- Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support (ACLS)
- Pediatric Advanced Life Support (PALS)
- Certified Critical Care Nurse (CCRN)
- Oncology Certified Nurse (OCN)
- Certified Emergency Nurse (CEN)

## Key Skills by Specialty

### Medical-Surgical
- Patient assessment and monitoring
- Wound care and dressing changes
- Post-operative care
- Pain management

### Emergency/Critical Care
- Triage and rapid assessment
- Trauma care
- Ventilator management
- Code team participation

### Pediatrics
- Child development knowledge
- Family-centered care
- Pediatric medication calculations
- Age-appropriate communication

## Strong Bullet Point Examples

Quantify your impact whenever possible:

- "Provided compassionate care to 15-20 patients daily in 40-bed medical-surgical unit"
- "Reduced medication errors by 25% through implementation of barcode scanning verification"
- "Achieved 98% patient satisfaction scores over 12-month period"
- "Precepted 12 new graduate nurses, with 100% passing NCLEX on first attempt"
- "Led quality improvement initiative reducing fall rates by 40% on unit"
- "Maintained 100% compliance with infection control protocols across 500+ patient encounters"

## Resume Formatting Tips

### What to Include
1. **Header** - Name, credentials (BSN, RN), contact info
2. **Licenses & Certifications** - Place prominently near top
3. **Professional Summary** - 3-4 lines highlighting expertise
4. **Clinical Experience** - Focus on relevant settings
5. **Education** - Include GPA if above 3.5
6. **Skills** - Both clinical and technical (EHR systems)

## Nurse Resume Format & Template Tips

Choosing the right resume format is critical for healthcare roles:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for nursing candidates with steady career progression, listing your most recent clinical experience first
- **Professional resume format** — Use clean section headers, consistent spacing, and a modern but readable font like Calibri or Arial
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Avoid graphics, tables, and columns that confuse applicant tracking systems. Stick to standard section headers
- **Resume length** — 1 page for entry-level / 1-2 pages for experienced / 2 pages for senior based on your experience level
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format across all devices and ATS platforms

### Common Mistakes
- Forgetting to list license numbers
- Using medical jargon without context
- Not highlighting certifications prominently
- Missing quantifiable patient care metrics
- Ignoring EHR system experience (Epic, Cerner, Meditech)
