## What Makes a Great Recreation Coordinator Resume?

Recreation coordinators plan and manage recreational programs and activities for communities. With +10% growth and $48,000 average salary, Recreation Coordinator positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated Recreation Coordinator with training in Program Planning and Event Management. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Recreation Coordinator with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Program Planning and Facility Coordination with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

Recreation Coordinator professionals earn approximately **$48,000** with **+10%** projected growth.
