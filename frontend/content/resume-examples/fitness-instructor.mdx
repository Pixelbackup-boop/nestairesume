## What Makes a Great Fitness Instructor Resume?

Fitness instructors lead group exercise classes in various formats and fitness levels. With +19% growth and $42,000 average salary, Fitness Instructor positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated Fitness Instructor with training in Group Exercise and Choreography. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Fitness Instructor with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Group Exercise and Music Selection with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

Fitness Instructor professionals earn approximately **$42,000** with **+19%** projected growth.
