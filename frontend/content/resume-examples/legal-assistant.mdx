## What Makes a Great Legal Assistant Resume?

This legal assistant resume example shows how to present your legal knowledge and organizational skills to law firms and corporate legal departments. The right resume format emphasizes your ability to manage caseloads, draft documents, and support attorneys efficiently. A strong resume example for legal assistants demonstrates attention to detail, familiarity with legal procedures, and the ability to handle confidential information. Your resume format should convey the precision and professionalism that legal employers expect from every document you produce.

## Professional Summary Examples

**For Entry-Level:**
"Detail-oriented Legal Assistant with paralegal certificate and 1 year of internship experience at a mid-size litigation firm. Assisted with discovery document review of 5,000+ pages and drafted 20+ motions and correspondence. Proficient in Westlaw, LexisNexis, and Clio with a professional resume focused on accuracy and efficiency."

**For Mid-Level:**
"Experienced Legal Assistant with 5 years supporting partners at a 50-attorney corporate law firm. Managed 30+ active case files simultaneously while coordinating depositions, court filings, and client communications. Reduced document preparation time by 25% through template standardization. Professional resume demonstrates expertise across litigation, real estate, and corporate law."

**For Senior:**
"Senior Legal Assistant with 10+ years of experience in complex commercial litigation and regulatory compliance. Supported lead counsel on cases valued at $50M+. Supervised team of 3 junior assistants and paralegals while managing e-discovery processes for 500,000+ document productions."

## Essential Skills to Highlight

### Legal Skills
- Legal research and analysis
- Document drafting (motions, briefs, contracts)
- Case management and file organization
- Court filing procedures (state and federal)
- Discovery and e-discovery support
- Client intake and communication

### Software & Technology
- Westlaw, LexisNexis
- Case management (Clio, MyCase, PracticePanther)
- E-discovery (Relativity, Concordance)
- Microsoft Office Suite (advanced Word formatting)
- Document management systems (iManage, NetDocuments)
- Court e-filing systems (CM/ECF, PACER)

### Certifications
- Paralegal Certificate (ABA-approved program)
- Certified Legal Assistant (CLA/CP)
- Notary Public
- NALA Advanced Paralegal Certification

Present your certifications prominently in your resume template, as many law firms require or prefer certified candidates.

## Legal Assistant Resume Format & Template Tips

Legal professionals expect polished, precise documents — your resume format should reflect that standard:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for legal assistants with progressive experience across practice areas
- **Clean resume format** — Use conservative fonts (Times New Roman, Garamond), clear headers, and consistent formatting befitting the legal industry
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Law firms use ATS software. Standard formatting with no graphics or columns ensures your resume parses correctly
- **Resume length** — 1 page for under 5 years of experience; up to 2 pages for senior legal assistants with diverse practice area experience
- **Practice area resume format** — Organize experience by practice area (litigation, corporate, real estate) if you have worked across multiple areas
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve the resume format — essential in the detail-oriented legal profession

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not specifying practice areas** — Litigation, corporate, family law, and real estate require different skills
2. **Missing deadline metrics** — Zero missed deadlines is a powerful statement for any legal assistant
3. **Ignoring technology** — Legal tech proficiency (Clio, Relativity, e-filing) is increasingly mandatory
4. **Generic formatting** — Legal employers expect precise, professional-looking documents
5. **Omitting confidentiality awareness** — Mention your ability to handle sensitive and privileged information

## ATS Optimization for Legal Assistant Resumes

Law firms and legal departments use applicant tracking systems to manage candidate pipelines. Ensure your ats resume format passes automated screening:
- Include legal-specific keywords: "legal research," "case management," "document drafting," "e-discovery," "court filing"
- Name your legal software in your ats resume template: "Westlaw," "LexisNexis," "Clio," "Relativity," "CM/ECF"
- Use both "legal assistant" and "paralegal" if the job posting uses either term interchangeably
- Spell out court names and jurisdictions: "U.S. District Court, Southern District of New York"
- Include practice area keywords that match the posting: "litigation," "corporate," "intellectual property," "real estate"
