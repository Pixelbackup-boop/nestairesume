## What Makes a Great ESL Teacher Resume?

ESL teachers help non-native speakers develop English language proficiency. With +12% growth and $56,000 average salary, ESL Teacher positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated ESL Teacher with training in English Instruction and Cultural Sensitivity. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled ESL Teacher with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in English Instruction and Assessment with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

ESL Teacher professionals earn approximately **$56,000** with **+12%** projected growth.

**Sources:** [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/education-training-and-library/adult-literacy-and-ged-teachers.htm), [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/), [PayScale](https://www.payscale.com/).
