## What Makes a Great Housekeeper Resume?

Housekeepers maintain the cleanliness that guests expect. Speed, attention to detail, and reliability are essential. With +6% growth and $30,000 average salary, Housekeeper roles offer solid hospitality careers.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Enthusiastic Housekeeper with training in Room Cleaning and Attention to Detail. Strong commitment to guest satisfaction and team success."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Housekeeper with 4+ years in hospitality. Proven record in Room Cleaning and Time Management with excellent guest feedback."

## Salary & Outlook

Housekeeper professionals earn approximately **$30,000** with **+6%** growth projected.

## Common Mistakes

### Not showing room quota

Rooms per day demonstrates productivity

### Missing quality record

## Hiring Manager Tip

> **Housekeeper resumes with guest satisfaction metrics and operational achievements get prioritized.**

"Housekeeper maintaining 16-room quota daily in luxury resort, consistently passing quality inspections. Zero guest complaints in 2 years." Show rooms per day, quality record, and property type.

## Interview Questions

### "How do you prioritize rooms during busy periods?"

Discuss checkouts, VIPs, and staying organized.

### "What do you do if you find something suspicious?"

Cover security awareness and proper reporting.
