## What Makes a Great Administrative Assistant Resume?

This administrative assistant resume example shows exactly what hiring managers want to see. The right resume format makes the difference between landing interviews and getting filtered out. A strong admin resume proves you keep operations running smoothly. Hiring managers look for organizational ability, multitasking skills, and tech proficiency. Quantify your impact — how many people you supported, how much you improved processes.

## Professional Summary Examples

**For Entry-Level:**
"Organized Administrative Assistant with 1 year of office experience supporting teams of 15+. Proficient in Microsoft Office Suite and Google Workspace. Managed scheduling for 3 managers while maintaining 100% appointment accuracy."

**For Mid-Level:**
"Administrative Professional with 4+ years supporting C-suite executives in fast-paced corporate environments. Coordinated 50+ meetings monthly, managed $30K travel budget, and implemented digital filing system reducing document retrieval time by 70%."

**For Executive Assistant:**
"Executive Assistant with 7+ years supporting Fortune 500 CEOs and Board of Directors. Managed complex international travel, board meeting logistics, and confidential correspondence. Known for anticipating needs and maintaining absolute discretion."

## Administrative Assistant Resume Format & Template Tips

Choosing the right resume format is critical for administrative and office support roles:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for administrative assistant candidates with steady career progression, listing your most recent office experience first
- **Professional resume format** — Use clean section headers, consistent spacing, and a modern but readable font like Calibri or Arial
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Avoid graphics, tables, and columns that confuse applicant tracking systems. Stick to standard section headers
- **Resume length** — 1 page for entry-level administrative assistants, extending to 1-2 pages for experienced executive assistants and office managers based on your experience level
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format across all devices and ATS platforms

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Underselling your role** — Administrative work has real business impact; quantify it
2. **Missing software skills** — List specific tools, not just "computer skills"
3. **No metrics** — Number of people supported, events planned, budget managed
4. **Too task-focused** — Show outcomes, not just responsibilities
5. **Poor formatting** — Your resume should reflect the organizational skills you claim
