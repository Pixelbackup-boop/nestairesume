## What Makes a Great Executive Assistant Resume?

This executive assistant resume example shows exactly what hiring managers want to see. The right resume format makes the difference between landing an interview and getting filtered out. Executive assistant resumes must convey trusted partnership with senior leaders, not just administrative task completion. The best EAs are force multipliers — your resume should show how you make executives more effective.

## Professional Summary Examples

**For Mid-Level EA:**
"Executive Assistant with 4 years supporting VP and SVP-level executives in Fortune 500 tech company. Managed complex international travel across 12 countries, coordinated 200+ meetings quarterly, and maintained $100K department budget."

**For Senior EA (C-Suite):**
"Senior Executive Assistant supporting CEO and CFO of $500M public company for 6+ years. Managed Board of Directors logistics, investor meeting preparation, and confidential M&A communications. Known for anticipating needs and maintaining absolute discretion."

**For Chief of Staff:**
"Chief of Staff with 8 years of progressive EA and operational experience. Manages CEO's $5M office budget, leads 3-person admin team, and drives strategic initiatives. Reduced executive meeting overhead by 35% through agenda optimization."

## Executive Assistant Resume Format & Template Tips

Choosing the right resume format is critical for administrative and executive support roles:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for executive assistant candidates with steady career progression, listing your most recent C-suite support experience first
- **Professional resume format** — Use clean section headers, consistent spacing, and a modern but readable font like Calibri or Arial
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Avoid graphics, tables, and columns that confuse applicant tracking systems. Stick to standard section headers
- **Resume length** — 1 page for entry-level / 1-2 pages for experienced / 2 pages for senior based on your experience level
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format across all devices and ATS platforms

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Underselling the role** — EA is a strategic role; position yourself as a business partner
2. **Missing executive level context** — Specify C-suite, VP, or director level
3. **No confidentiality indicators** — Discretion is the top EA requirement
4. **Listing tasks instead of impact** — Show how you multiplied executive effectiveness
5. **Ignoring technology skills** — Modern EAs need strong tech proficiency
