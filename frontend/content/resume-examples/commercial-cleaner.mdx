## What Makes a Great Commercial Cleaner Resume?

A strong commercial cleaner resume demonstrates your ability to deliver results in the cleaning field. Hiring managers in this industry specifically look for candidates who can show measurable impact in floor care and sanitization.

The most effective commercial cleaner resumes go beyond listing job duties. They showcase quantifiable achievements that prove you can excel in this role.


Commercial cleaning contracts require reliability documentation and scope of work capability. Your resume should specify square footage maintained per shift, types of facilities serviced (medical, office, industrial), and certifications in specialized cleaning methods like floor care, carpet extraction, or post-construction cleanup.

## Professional Summary Examples

### Entry-Level Commercial Cleaner
Motivated professional with foundational training in floor care and sanitization. Currently pursuing OSHA 10 certification. Strong work ethic with demonstrated ability to learn quickly and contribute to team success.

### Mid-Level Commercial Cleaner
Experienced commercial cleaner with 0-2 years delivering results in floor care and sanitization. Proven track record of exceeding performance targets while maintaining high standards. Seeking to leverage expertise in a challenging new role.

### Senior Commercial Cleaner
Accomplished commercial cleaner professional with extensive experience leading initiatives in floor care. Known for developing innovative solutions that improve efficiency and drive results. OSHA 10 certified with expertise mentoring junior team members.

## Commercial Cleaner Resume Format & Template Tips

Your commercial cleaner resume should emphasize relevant experience prominently. Consider these formatting recommendations:

- **Lead with impact:** Open with a summary highlighting your strongest cleaning achievements
- **Quantify results:** Include specific metrics like percentages, dollar amounts, or volume handled
- **Highlight certifications:** Feature credentials like OSHA 10 prominently
- **Use industry keywords:** Include terms like "commercial cleaner", "janitorial", "floor care" for ATS optimization
- **Keep it concise:** One page for entry-level, two pages maximum for experienced professionals

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Focusing on duties instead of achievements** - Describe what you accomplished, not just what you were responsible for
2. **Missing relevant keywords** - Include industry terms like commercial cleaner, janitorial, floor care for ATS screening
3. **Omitting certifications** - Credentials like OSHA 10 can set you apart
4. **Using generic descriptions** - Be specific about your contributions and their impact
5. **Neglecting soft skills** - Cleaning roles require strong interpersonal abilities alongside technical skills

## ATS Optimization for Commercial Cleaner Resumes

Ensure your resume passes Applicant Tracking Systems by including these keywords naturally throughout your document:

**Priority Keywords:** commercial cleaner, janitorial, floor care, sanitization

**Additional Keywords:** OSHA, facility maintenance, industrial cleaning

Format your resume as a single-column layout without tables, text boxes, or graphics. Use standard section headings that ATS parsers recognize: Professional Summary, Work Experience, Education, Skills, and Certifications. Save as PDF unless the application specifically requests .docx format. Ensure your contact information appears in the body of the resume, not in headers or footers that some ATS systems skip during parsing.

Match the exact phrasing from job descriptions when possible, but maintain natural readability.
