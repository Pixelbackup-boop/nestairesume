## What Makes a Great Elementary Teacher Resume?

Elementary teachers educate children in foundational subjects and skills. The role requires patience, creativity, and classroom management ability. With +4% growth and $62,000 average salary, Elementary Teacher positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated Elementary Teacher with training in Lesson Planning and Classroom Management. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Elementary Teacher with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Lesson Planning and Differentiated Instruction with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

Elementary Teacher professionals earn approximately **$62,000** with **+4%** projected growth.

**Sources:** [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/education-training-and-library/kindergarten-and-elementary-school-teachers.htm), [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/), [PayScale](https://www.payscale.com/).
