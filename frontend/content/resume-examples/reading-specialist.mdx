## What Makes a Great Reading Specialist Resume?

Reading specialists help struggling readers develop literacy skills and coach teachers. With +4% growth and $58,000 average salary, Reading Specialist positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated Reading Specialist with training in Reading Assessment and Intervention. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Reading Specialist with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Reading Assessment and Phonics Instruction with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

Reading Specialist professionals earn approximately **$58,000** with **+4%** projected growth.

**Sources:** [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/education-training-and-library/special-education-teachers.htm), [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/), [PayScale](https://www.payscale.com/).
