## What Makes a Great Front Desk Agent Resume?

Front desk agents are the face of the hotel, creating first and last impressions. Multi-tasking and problem-solving are essential. With +9% growth and $32,000 average salary, Front Desk Agent roles offer solid hospitality careers.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Enthusiastic Front Desk Agent with training in Guest Check-in/out and Reservations. Strong commitment to guest satisfaction and team success."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Front Desk Agent with 4+ years in hospitality. Proven record in Guest Check-in/out and PMS Systems with excellent guest feedback."

## Salary & Outlook

Front Desk Agent professionals earn approximately **$32,000** with **+9%** growth projected.

## Common Mistakes

### Not mentioning PMS experience

Opera, OnQ, FOSSE—list specific systems

### Missing guest satisfaction data

Scores, reviews, recognition demonstrate service quality



## Hiring Manager Tip

> **Front Desk Agent resumes with guest satisfaction metrics and operational achievements get prioritized.**

"Front desk agent at 350-room convention hotel, handling 150+ check-ins daily with 95% guest satisfaction. Top performer in loyalty enrollments." Show volume, guest scores, and sales achievements.

## Interview Questions

### "How do you handle an overbooked night?"

Discuss walking guests, compensation, and maintaining satisfaction.

### "Describe your upselling approach"

Show natural, guest-focused sales technique.
