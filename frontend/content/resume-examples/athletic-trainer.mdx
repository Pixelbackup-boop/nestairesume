## What Makes a Great Athletic Trainer Resume?

Athletic trainers prevent and treat injuries for athletes, providing emergency care and rehabilitation. With +17% growth and $50,000 average salary, Athletic Trainer positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated Athletic Trainer with training in Injury Prevention and Emergency Care. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Athletic Trainer with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Injury Prevention and Rehabilitation with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

Athletic Trainer professionals earn approximately **$50,000** with **+17%** projected growth.
