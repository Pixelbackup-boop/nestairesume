## What Makes a Great Court Reporter Resume?

Court reporters create verbatim records of legal proceedings. Speed, accuracy, and certification are essential. With +3% growth and $62,000 average salary, Court Reporter positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated Court Reporter with training in Stenography and Real-time Reporting. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Court Reporter with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Stenography and Legal Terminology with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

Court Reporter professionals earn approximately **$62,000** with **+3%** projected growth.
