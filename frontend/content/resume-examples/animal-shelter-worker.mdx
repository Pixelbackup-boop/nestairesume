## What Makes a Great Animal Shelter Worker Resume?

Animal shelter workers care for homeless animals and help match them with adoptive families. With +22% growth and $28,000 average salary, Animal Shelter Worker positions offer solid career opportunities.

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated Animal Shelter Worker with training in Animal Care and Cleaning. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Animal Shelter Worker with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Animal Care and Adoption Counseling with excellent results."
