## What Makes a Great Logistics Coordinator Resume?

This Logistics Coordinator resume example shows exactly what hiring managers want to see. The right resume format makes the difference between landing an interview and getting filtered out. Logistics Coordinators keep supply chains moving, and your resume must show that you can manage shipments, coordinate carriers, track inventory, and solve problems under tight deadlines. Employers look for candidates who can reduce shipping costs, improve delivery times, and maintain accurate records.

## Professional Summary Examples

**For Entry-Level:**
"Detail-oriented Logistics Coordinator with 1 year of experience managing inbound and outbound shipments for a regional distribution center. Coordinated 50+ daily shipments across LTL and FTL carriers, maintaining a 98% on-time delivery rate. Proficient in SAP, ShipStation, and Excel-based inventory tracking."

**For Mid-Level:**
"Efficient Logistics Coordinator with 4+ years of experience managing end-to-end shipping operations for a $20M annual freight budget. Coordinate 200+ weekly shipments across domestic and international routes with 99.1% on-time delivery. Reduced freight costs by 15% through carrier negotiations and route optimization."

**For Senior:**
"Senior Logistics Coordinator with 8+ years of experience in multi-modal transportation management, warehouse operations, and international trade compliance. Manage logistics for 500+ weekly shipments across 30 countries, overseeing a $40M annual freight spend. Implemented a TMS platform that reduced manual data entry by 70% and improved shipment visibility for 200+ customers."

## Essential Skills to Highlight

### Shipping & Transportation
- LTL, FTL, and parcel shipment coordination
- Carrier selection and rate negotiation
- International freight forwarding (air, ocean, ground)
- Customs documentation and import/export compliance
- Route planning and optimization
- Freight claims and damage resolution

### Inventory & Warehouse
- Inventory tracking and cycle counting
- Warehouse management system operations
- Order picking, packing, and shipping verification
- Receiving and put-away coordination
- Safety stock and reorder point management
- Returns processing and reverse logistics

### Operations & Technology
- ERP systems (SAP, Oracle, NetSuite)
- Transportation management systems (TMS)
- Microsoft Excel (VLOOKUP, pivot tables, macros)
- Shipment tracking and reporting dashboards
- Vendor and carrier relationship management
- Cross-functional coordination with sales and production

## Logistics Coordinator Resume Format & Template Tips

Choosing the right resume format is critical for logistics roles:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for Logistics Coordinator candidates with steady career progression in supply chain and shipping
- **Professional resume format** — Use clean section headers, consistent spacing, and a modern but readable font
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Avoid graphics, tables, and columns that confuse applicant tracking systems
- **Resume length** — 1 page for entry-level / 1-2 pages for experienced / 2 pages for senior
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format across all devices and ATS platforms

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not quantifying shipment volumes** — Daily and weekly shipment counts demonstrate the scale of your operational responsibility
2. **Omitting cost savings** — Logistics is about efficiency; show how you reduced freight costs, transit times, or error rates
3. **Ignoring technology proficiency** — ERP, TMS, and Excel skills are expected and must be listed explicitly
4. **Being vague about geography** — Specify whether you manage domestic, international, or multi-modal logistics
5. **Forgetting compliance experience** — Customs documentation, hazmat handling, and regulatory compliance are valuable differentiators
