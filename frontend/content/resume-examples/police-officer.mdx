## What Makes a Great Police Officer Resume?

Police officers maintain public safety, investigate crimes, and serve their communities. With +3% growth and $65,000 average salary, Police Officer positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated Police Officer with training in Law Enforcement and Investigation. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Police Officer with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Law Enforcement and Communication with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

Police Officer professionals earn approximately **$65,000** with **+3%** projected growth.

**Sources:** [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/protective-service/police-and-detectives.htm), [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/), [PayScale](https://www.payscale.com/).
