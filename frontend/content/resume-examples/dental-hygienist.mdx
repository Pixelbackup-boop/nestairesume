## What Makes a Great Dental Hygienist Resume?

Dental hygienists provide preventive oral care and patient education, working closely with dentists to maintain patient oral health. The role requires clinical precision, patient communication skills, and the ability to work efficiently within appointment time constraints.

Healthcare employers evaluate Dental Hygienist candidates on specific credentials, clinical competencies, and demonstrated outcomes. With +9% projected job growth and an average salary of $82,000, competition for top Dental Hygienist positions requires a resume that immediately communicates your qualifications and value. This guide covers the specific sections, metrics, and formatting that healthcare recruiters look for when reviewing Dental Hygienist applications.

## Professional Summary Examples

**For Entry-Level Dental Hygienist:**
"Recently certified Dental Hygienist with hands-on clinical experience in teeth cleaning and periodontal assessment. Completed training with focus on x-rays and patient-centered care. RDH License certified with strong foundation in healthcare protocols and safety procedures."

**For Experienced Dental Hygienist:**
"Dedicated Dental Hygienist with 5+ years of experience in teeth cleaning and periodontal assessment. Consistently maintained high patient satisfaction scores while managing complex cases. Proficient in scaling and root planing with proven track record of quality outcomes and team collaboration."

**For Senior Dental Hygienist:**
"Accomplished Dental Hygienist with 10+ years of progressive experience leading teeth cleaning initiatives. Expert in x-rays with demonstrated success improving departmental outcomes by 25%. Mentor to junior staff with comprehensive knowledge of patient education and regulatory compliance."

## Dental Hygienist Resume Format & Template Tips

Dental Hygienist resumes require specific attention to credentials and clinical experience. Here are formatting guidelines tailored to this role:

- **Credentials first** — List your RDH License, State Dental Hygiene License immediately after your name. Healthcare recruiters verify credentials before reading anything else.
- **Quantify your experience** — Include patient volumes, success rates, and quality metrics. Numbers demonstrate competence better than generic descriptions.
- **Highlight relevant certifications** — Create a dedicated section for certifications with expiration dates. Keep them current and prominently displayed.
- **Use clinical terminology** — Mirror the language from job postings. Include specific equipment, systems, and protocols you're trained on.
- **One page for <5 years experience** — Focus on your most relevant and recent experience. Senior professionals may use two pages for extensive accomplishments.
- **Include continuing education** — Healthcare evolves rapidly. Show your commitment to staying current with recent training and certifications.

## Hiring Manager Tip

> **Hygienist resumes should highlight your RDH license, daily patient volume, and any expanded functions (local anesthesia, nitrous oxide).**

 Include your periodontal therapy experience, production numbers if applicable, and patient retention rates. Mention specific software systems and digital radiography experience.

## Common Dental Hygienist Interview Questions

Preparing for interviews is essential for landing your Dental Hygienist role. Here are questions frequently asked in Dental Hygienist interviews:

### "Describe your approach to a patient with severe periodontal disease."

Discuss assessment, treatment planning, SRP technique, and maintenance schedule.

### "How do you handle a patient who is anxious about dental treatment?"

Describe communication techniques, pacing, and creating a comfortable environment.

### "What is your experience with local anesthesia administration?"

Discuss your training, techniques, and managing complications.

### "How do you stay productive while maintaining quality care?"

Explain time management, efficiency techniques, and prioritization.

### "Describe your patient education approach."

Discuss customizing instructions, motivational techniques, and follow-up.

## ATS Optimization for Dental Hygienist Resumes

Healthcare ATS systems screen for specific credentials, certifications, and clinical keywords. Missing key terms can disqualify an otherwise strong application.

- Include exact credential abbreviations: "RDH License", "State Dental Hygiene License"
- Use clinical terminology from job postings: "dental hygienist", "RDH", "oral hygiene", "periodontal", "scaling"
- List specific equipment, systems, or protocols you're experienced with
- Include both full terms and abbreviations for searchability
- Mention specific patient populations and care settings
