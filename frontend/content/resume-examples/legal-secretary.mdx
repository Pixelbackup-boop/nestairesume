## What Makes a Great Legal Secretary Resume?

Legal secretaries manage attorney schedules, prepare documents, and maintain client relationships. Organization and legal terminology knowledge are essential. With +4% growth and $52,000 average salary, Legal Secretary positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated Legal Secretary with training in Legal Documentation and Calendar Management. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Legal Secretary with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Legal Documentation and Court Filings with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

Legal Secretary professionals earn approximately **$52,000** with **+4%** projected growth.
