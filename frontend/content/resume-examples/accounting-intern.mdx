## What Makes a Great Accounting Intern Resume?

This accounting intern resume example shows exactly what hiring managers want to see. The right resume format makes the difference between landing an interview and getting filtered out. As an accounting intern, you may not have years of experience, but you can demonstrate strong academic performance, relevant coursework, and any practical exposure to accounting tools. Employers hiring interns want to see a willingness to learn, attention to detail, and foundational knowledge of accounting principles. Highlight class projects, volunteer bookkeeping, or campus organization treasurer roles to show real-world application.

## Professional Summary Examples

**For Entry-Level:**
"Accounting student pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Accounting with a 3.7 GPA, seeking an internship to apply classroom knowledge to real-world financial operations. Completed coursework in Financial Accounting, Managerial Accounting, and Taxation. Proficient in Excel and QuickBooks with hands-on experience from a campus bookkeeping volunteer role processing 50+ monthly transactions."

**For Mid-Level:**
"Junior-year Accounting major with one prior internship at a regional CPA firm where I assisted with 40+ individual tax returns and reconciled 8 client bank accounts monthly. Earned QuickBooks Certified User designation and maintained a 3.8 GPA while serving as Treasurer for the university's Business Student Association, managing a $12,000 annual budget."

**For Senior:**
"Senior Accounting student with two internship experiences across public accounting and corporate finance. Assisted audit teams in reviewing $5M+ in client financials, prepared 75+ tax returns, and developed Excel-based reconciliation templates adopted firm-wide. On track for CPA eligibility upon graduation with 150 credit hours completed."

## Essential Skills to Highlight

### Academic & Technical Skills
- **GAAP Principles** — Understanding of Generally Accepted Accounting Principles from coursework
- **Financial Statements** — Ability to read and prepare balance sheets, income statements, and cash flow statements
- **Tax Fundamentals** — Exposure to individual and business tax preparation concepts
- **Bookkeeping** — Recording transactions, maintaining journals, and posting to ledgers
- **Cost Accounting** — Understanding of cost allocation, budgeting, and variance analysis
- **Audit Basics** — Familiarity with audit procedures and internal control frameworks

### Software Skills
- **Microsoft Excel** — Formulas, pivot tables, VLOOKUP, and basic macros for financial analysis
- **QuickBooks** — Data entry, invoice creation, and basic reporting
- **Sage / Xero** — Exposure to cloud accounting platforms through coursework or projects
- **Microsoft Office** — Word for document preparation, PowerPoint for presentations
- **Google Workspace** — Sheets and Docs for collaborative work
- **TurboTax / Tax Software** — Basic experience with tax preparation tools

### Soft Skills
- **Attention to Detail** — Catching errors in data entry and financial calculations
- **Eagerness to Learn** — Proactively seeking feedback and developing new competencies
- **Organization** — Managing coursework, part-time jobs, and extracurricular commitments
- **Communication** — Presenting financial information clearly to professors and peers
- **Integrity** — Understanding the ethical standards expected in the accounting profession
- **Teamwork** — Collaborating on group projects and contributing to team goals

## Accounting Intern Resume Format & Template Tips

Choosing the right resume format is critical for finance roles:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for accounting intern candidates with steady career progression, listing your most recent education and experience first
- **Professional resume format** — Use clean section headers, consistent spacing, and a modern but readable font like Calibri or Arial
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Avoid graphics, tables, and columns that confuse applicant tracking systems. Stick to standard section headers
- **Resume length** — 1 page for entry-level / 1-2 pages for experienced / 2 pages for senior based on your experience level
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format across all devices and ATS platforms

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Hiding your GPA** — If it is above 3.0, include it prominently since employers weigh academic performance heavily for interns
2. **Ignoring relevant coursework** — List specific accounting courses like Intermediate Accounting, Auditing, and Tax to show your knowledge base
3. **Leaving out campus involvement** — Treasurer roles, accounting club membership, and VITA volunteer experience all count
4. **Not mentioning CPA eligibility** — If you are on track for 150 credit hours, state it clearly as it signals long-term commitment
5. **Writing a generic objective** — Tailor your summary to each firm, mentioning the company name and specific role you are applying for
