## What Makes a Great Hotel Manager Resume?

This hotel manager resume example shows exactly what hiring managers want to see. The right resume format makes the difference between landing interviews and getting filtered out. A winning hotel manager resume combines hospitality passion with business acumen — employers want to see guest satisfaction scores, revenue growth numbers, and team leadership results. Your resume example must prove you can run a profitable operation while delivering exceptional guest experiences.

## Professional Summary Examples

**For Entry-Level:**
"Hospitality Management graduate with 2 years of front desk and guest services experience at a 200-room upscale hotel. Achieved 95% guest satisfaction rating, upsold room upgrades generating $15K in additional monthly revenue, and trained 4 new team members. Professional resume reflects strong customer focus and operational knowledge."

**For Mid-Level:**
"Hotel Manager with 6 years of progressive hospitality experience managing a 150-room boutique property. Increased RevPAR by 18%, achieved a 4.7-star TripAdvisor rating, and reduced staff turnover by 25% through improved training programs. Professional resume demonstrates expertise in revenue management and team development."

**For Senior:**
"General Manager with 12+ years overseeing full-service hotels with 300+ rooms and $10M+ annual revenue. Led property renovation achieving 22% rate increase, maintained 92% occupancy year-round, and managed teams of 80+ across front office, F&B, housekeeping, and maintenance departments."

## Hotel Manager Resume Format & Template Tips

Choosing the right resume format is critical for hospitality management roles:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for hotel manager candidates with steady career progression, listing your most recent hospitality experience first. This resume format clearly shows growth from front desk to management
- **Professional resume format** — Use clean section headers, consistent spacing, and a modern but readable font like Calibri or Arial
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Avoid graphics, tables, and columns that confuse applicant tracking systems. Stick to standard section headers
- **Resume length** — 1-2 pages depending on experience; include only the most relevant hotel and hospitality roles
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format across all devices and ATS platforms

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **No revenue metrics** — Always include RevPAR, occupancy rates, ADR, and total revenue managed
2. **Missing guest satisfaction scores** — TripAdvisor, Google, or internal NPS scores prove your service quality
3. **Vague team leadership** — Specify team sizes, departments managed, and turnover improvements
4. **Ignoring brand standards** — If you managed a branded property (Marriott, Hilton, IHG), mention brand compliance
5. **Overlooking technology** — PMS systems (Opera, Cloudbeds), channel managers, and booking engines are must-mention skills

## ATS Optimization for Hotel Manager Resumes

To pass applicant tracking systems with your ats resume format, follow these guidelines:
- Include exact keywords from the job posting: "guest relations," "revenue management," "staff leadership," "budget management," "housekeeping"
- Use an ats resume template with standard section headers — "Experience," "Skills," "Education," "Certifications"
- Mention specific PMS platforms (Opera PMS, Cloudbeds, RoomKey) that ATS systems scan for
- Spell out abbreviations at first use: "Revenue Per Available Room (RevPAR)," "Average Daily Rate (ADR)"
- Include hospitality certifications: CHA, CHIA, or ServSafe to boost ATS match scores
