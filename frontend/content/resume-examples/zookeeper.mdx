## What Makes a Great Zookeeper Resume?

Zookeepers care for animals in zoos, providing enrichment and educating the public. With +22% growth and $32,000 average salary, Zookeeper positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated Zookeeper with training in Animal Husbandry and Feeding. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Zookeeper with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Animal Husbandry and Enrichment with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

Zookeeper professionals earn approximately **$32,000** with **+22%** projected growth.
