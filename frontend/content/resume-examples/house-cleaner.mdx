## What Makes a Great House Cleaner Resume?

House cleaners maintain cleanliness in private homes for residential clients. With +6% growth and $30,000 average salary, House Cleaner positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated House Cleaner with training in Cleaning and Organization. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled House Cleaner with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Cleaning and Attention to Detail with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

House Cleaner professionals earn approximately **$30,000** with **+6%** projected growth.
