## What Makes a Great High School Teacher Resume?

High school teachers instruct students in specific subjects, preparing them for college and careers. With +4% growth and $64,000 average salary, High School Teacher positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated High School Teacher with training in Subject Expertise and Lesson Planning. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled High School Teacher with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Subject Expertise and Assessment with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

High School Teacher professionals earn approximately **$64,000** with **+4%** projected growth.
