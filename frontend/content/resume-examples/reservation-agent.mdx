## What Makes a Great Reservation Agent Resume?

Reservation agents convert inquiries into bookings. Phone presence, product knowledge, and sales ability drive success. With +9% growth and $35,000 average salary, Reservation Agent roles offer solid hospitality careers.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Enthusiastic Reservation Agent with training in Phone Skills and Reservation Systems. Strong commitment to guest satisfaction and team success."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Reservation Agent with 4+ years in hospitality. Proven record in Phone Skills and Upselling with excellent guest feedback."

## Salary & Outlook

Reservation Agent professionals earn approximately **$35,000** with **+9%** growth projected.

> **Reservation Agent resumes with guest satisfaction metrics and operational achievements get prioritized.**

"Reservation agent handling 80+ calls daily with 45% conversion rate and $150 average booking value. Top performer in upselling and loyalty enrollments." Show call volume, conversion rates, and sales metrics.
