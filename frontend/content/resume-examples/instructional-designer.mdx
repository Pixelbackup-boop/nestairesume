## What Makes a Great Instructional Designer Resume?

Instructional designers create effective learning experiences for corporate and educational settings. With +10% growth and $72,000 average salary, Instructional Designer positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated Instructional Designer with training in Course Design and E-Learning. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Instructional Designer with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Course Design and Learning Management Systems with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

Instructional Designer professionals earn approximately **$72,000** with **+10%** projected growth.
