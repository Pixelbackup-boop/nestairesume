## What Makes a Great Real Estate Appraiser Resume?

Real estate appraisers determine property values for sales, mortgages, and tax purposes. With +5% growth and $65,000 average salary, Real Estate Appraiser positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated Real Estate Appraiser with training in Property Valuation and Market Analysis. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Real Estate Appraiser with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Property Valuation and Report Writing with excellent results."
