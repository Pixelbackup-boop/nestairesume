## What Makes a Great Material Handler Resume?

This material handler resume example shows exactly what hiring managers want to see. The right resume format makes the difference between landing an interview and getting filtered out. Material handlers are the backbone of warehouse and distribution operations, responsible for moving, storing, and tracking products accurately and safely. Your resume needs to prove you are efficient, reliable, and safety-conscious with the equipment certifications to back it up.

## Professional Summary Examples

**For Entry-Level:**
"Hardworking material handler with 6 months of seasonal warehouse experience at a major e-commerce fulfillment center. Picked and packed an average of 180 orders per shift with a 99.2% accuracy rate. Forklift certified (sit-down and reach truck) with OSHA 10 safety training completed."

**For Mid-Level:**
"Experienced Material Handler with 4 years in high-volume distribution environments handling receiving, put-away, picking, and shipping operations. Consistently ranked in the top 10% of warehouse associates for pick rate accuracy and speed. Proficient with WMS platforms including Manhattan and SAP, and certified on 4 types of powered industrial trucks."

**For Senior:**
"Reliable Material Handler with 9 years of warehouse and logistics experience, including 3 years as a lead material handler overseeing a 10-person team on the night shift. Trained 25+ new hires on equipment operation and safety protocols. Maintained zero OSHA recordable incidents over 4 consecutive years while handling an average daily volume of 12,000 units."

## Essential Skills to Highlight

### Equipment & Operations
- Forklift operation (sit-down, reach, order picker)
- Pallet jack (electric and manual)
- RF barcode scanners
- Conveyor systems
- Shrink-wrapping and palletizing
- Loading and unloading trucks

### Inventory & Systems
- Warehouse management systems (WMS)
- Cycle counting and inventory audits
- Shipping and receiving documentation
- Bill of lading processing
- Lot and serial number tracking
- FIFO and LIFO inventory methods

### Safety & Physical Skills
- OSHA safety compliance
- Hazardous material handling
- Personal protective equipment usage
- Heavy lifting (up to 50-75 lbs regularly)
- Dock safety and trailer securing
- Spill containment procedures

- "Reorganized warehouse bin locations based on velocity analysis, reducing average pick path distance by 25% and increasing team pick rate by 15%"
- "Maintained zero safety incidents over 3 years while handling hazardous materials including chemicals and compressed gases in a temperature-controlled warehouse"

## Material Handler Resume Format & Template Tips

Choosing the right resume format is critical for logistics and warehouse roles:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for material handler candidates with steady career progression showing increasing volume, equipment certifications, and leadership
- **Professional resume format** — Use clean section headers, consistent spacing, and a modern but readable font
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Avoid graphics, tables, and columns that confuse applicant tracking systems
- **Resume length** — 1 page for entry-level / 1-2 pages for experienced / 2 pages for senior
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format across all devices and ATS platforms

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not listing equipment certifications** — Forklift and powered industrial truck certifications are often mandatory; list every type you are certified on
2. **Being vague about volume** — Specify daily order counts, pallet quantities, and SKU ranges to demonstrate your capacity
3. **Ignoring accuracy metrics** — Pick accuracy rates and inventory accuracy percentages are critical; always include them
4. **Forgetting safety accomplishments** — A clean safety record is a major advantage; quantify the years and incidents avoided
5. **Leaving out WMS and technology experience** — Modern warehouses rely on technology; name the specific systems and scanners you have used
