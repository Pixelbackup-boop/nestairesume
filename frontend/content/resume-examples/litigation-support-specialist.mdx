## What Makes a Great Litigation Support Specialist Resume?

Litigation support specialists manage electronic discovery and trial preparation technology. Tech skills and legal knowledge are essential. With +4% growth and $60,000 average salary, Litigation Support Specialist positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated Litigation Support Specialist with training in E-Discovery and Document Review. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Litigation Support Specialist with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in E-Discovery and Technology with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

Litigation Support Specialist professionals earn approximately **$60,000** with **+4%** projected growth.
