## What Makes a Great Postal Worker Resume?

Postal workers sort and deliver mail, serving as essential community connectors. With -4% growth and $48,000 average salary, Postal Worker positions offer solid career opportunities.

**Experienced:** "Skilled Postal Worker with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Mail Processing and Physical Stamina with excellent results."
