## What Makes a Great Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN) Resume?

A strong LPN resume example demonstrates clinical competence, patient-centered care, and the reliability that healthcare facilities depend on. The ideal resume format for LPN positions puts licensure, clinical skills, and care setting experience front and center. This resume example goes beyond listing nursing duties — it highlights patient outcomes, care volumes, and specialized competencies that set you apart. An effective resume format for healthcare roles makes it easy for nurse managers and recruiters to quickly verify your licensure, certifications, and the types of patient populations you have served.

## Essential Skills to Highlight

Build your LPN resume template with skills that match what healthcare employers prioritize.

### Clinical Skills
- Comprehensive patient assessment and vital signs monitoring
- Medication administration (oral, IM, SQ, IV push, IV piggyback)
- Wound care: assessment, cleaning, dressing, and documentation
- Blood glucose monitoring and insulin administration
- Catheter insertion, care, and removal

### Patient Care
- Patient and family education on care plans and medications
- Activities of daily living (ADL) assistance and supervision for your resume template
- Fall prevention assessment and intervention
- Pain management assessment and non-pharmacological interventions
- End-of-life and palliative care support

### Documentation & Compliance
- Electronic Health Records: Epic, Cerner, PointClickCare, MatrixCare
- Accurate and timely clinical documentation and charting
- HIPAA compliance and patient privacy protection
- State and federal regulatory survey readiness
- Infection control protocols and PPE compliance

## Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN) Resume Format & Template Tips

The right resume format positions your nursing career for maximum impact:

- **Chronological resume format** — Best for LPNs with steady clinical employment, showing progression from new graduate to experienced nurse or charge nurse role
- **Clinical-focused resume format** — Lead with a "Licensure & Certifications" section at the top, followed by clinical skills, then work history using this resume format to help nurse managers quickly verify your credentials
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Use clean formatting with standard section headers. Healthcare organizations and staffing agencies use ATS software extensively; avoid graphics, columns, or decorative elements
- **Resume length** — 1 page is standard for LPNs at all experience levels; focus on your most recent 2-3 clinical positions
- Always include your license number (or "available upon request"), state of licensure, and expiration date in your resume format header or credentials section

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not listing licensure and certifications first** — Your LPN license, CPR/BLS, IV certification, and any specialty credentials should be the first things visible on your resume
2. **Being vague about patient populations** — Specify care settings (skilled nursing, rehab, home health), patient volumes, and acuity levels to give context to your experience
3. **Omitting EHR system names** — Epic, Cerner, PointClickCare, and MatrixCare are not interchangeable; list the specific platforms you have documented in
4. **Forgetting quality metrics** — Medication error rates, fall rates, infection rates, and survey compliance scores demonstrate clinical excellence; include them
5. **Not mentioning team supervision** — If you have supervised CNAs, trained new hires, or served as charge nurse, highlight these leadership experiences prominently

## ATS Optimization for Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN) Resumes

Healthcare organizations and nursing staffing agencies use applicant tracking systems to match candidates to specific clinical requirements. Build your resume with an ats resume format that mirrors the job posting language exactly. Include keywords like "patient assessment," "medication administration," "wound care," "vital signs," "patient education," "IV therapy," "EHR documentation," and "infection control" throughout your experience section. Use an ats resume template with single-column formatting, standard section headers, and no tables or images. Spell out abbreviations on first use (e.g., "Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN)") and include both the abbreviation and full term so the ATS captures every relevant qualification.
