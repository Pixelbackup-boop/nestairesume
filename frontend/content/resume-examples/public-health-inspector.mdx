## What Makes a Great Public Health Inspector Resume?

Public health inspectors ensure food establishments and facilities meet health standards. With +5% growth and $55,000 average salary, Public Health Inspector positions offer solid career opportunities.

**Experienced:** "Skilled Public Health Inspector with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Health Code Enforcement and Investigation with excellent results."
