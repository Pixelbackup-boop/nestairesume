## What Makes a Great Customer Success Manager Resume?

This customer success manager resume example shows what hiring managers look for in client-facing leadership candidates. The right resume format separates high-performing CSMs from the rest of the applicant pool. Customer success is about driving retention, expansion, and advocacy, so your resume example must demonstrate measurable improvements in churn reduction, net revenue retention, and customer satisfaction. Hiring managers scan for proof that you can manage a book of business, identify at-risk accounts, and drive product adoption. A strong resume format puts your retention rates and expansion revenue front and center, using a clean layout that highlights NRR percentages, customer health improvements, and upsell revenue. Every resume example that lands interviews leads with outcome-driven metrics rather than routine account management duties.

## Customer Success Manager Resume Format & Template Tips

Selecting the right resume format is essential for customer success roles:

- **Chronological resume format** — Best for CSMs with progressive account management experience, showing how your book of business, retention metrics, and strategic responsibilities have expanded over time
- **Professional resume format** — Use a polished layout with clear section headers for Summary, Experience, Skills, and Education. Consistent formatting signals the organizational skills customer-facing roles demand
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Avoid complex graphics, tables, or multi-column layouts that applicant tracking systems cannot parse. Use standard headings and clean formatting
- **Resume length** — 1 page for CSMs with under 5 years of experience; 1-2 pages for senior managers with extensive portfolio management and leadership accomplishments
- **Lead with retention metrics** — Place your biggest NRR percentages, expansion revenue, and retention rates near the top of each role. This resume format ensures key achievements catch the eye immediately
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format across all platforms

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **No retention or revenue metrics** — Customer success is metric-driven; a resume without NRR, churn rates, or expansion revenue will be overlooked
2. **Confusing customer success with customer support** — Emphasize strategic outcomes (retention, growth, adoption) rather than reactive ticket resolution
3. **Missing portfolio context** — Always specify your book of business size (ARR), number of accounts, and account tier (enterprise, mid-market, SMB)
4. **Ignoring cross-functional work** — CSMs collaborate with product, sales, and engineering; highlight these partnerships and their outcomes
5. **Generic skills section** — Tailor your skills to the specific job posting; include the exact CS platforms and tools mentioned

## ATS Optimization for Customer Success Manager Resumes

To ensure your resume passes applicant tracking systems, follow this ats resume format guidance:

- Include keywords like "customer success," "net revenue retention," "account management," "customer onboarding," and "churn reduction" naturally throughout your resume
- Use an ats resume template with standard section headings: Professional Summary, Work Experience, Skills, Education
- Mirror the exact job title and key phrases from the posting — if it says "Enterprise Customer Success Manager," use that phrase
- Avoid headers-only or graphics-only sections that ATS software cannot read
- Include both spelled-out terms and abbreviations (e.g., "Net Revenue Retention (NRR)")
- Place your most important keywords in the top third of your resume where ATS algorithms weight content more heavily
