## What Makes a Great Window Cleaner Resume?

Window cleaners maintain building windows, often working at heights. With +6% growth and $35,000 average salary, Window Cleaner positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated Window Cleaner with training in Window Cleaning and Height Safety. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Window Cleaner with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Window Cleaning and Equipment Use with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

Window Cleaner professionals earn approximately **$35,000** with **+6%** projected growth.
