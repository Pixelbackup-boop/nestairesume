## What Makes a Great Claims Adjuster Resume?

This claims adjuster resume example shows exactly what hiring managers want to see. The right resume format makes the difference between landing an interview and getting filtered out. A strong claims adjuster resume demonstrates investigative rigor, sharp negotiation skills, and the ability to handle a high volume of claims accurately and efficiently. Employers look for candidates who balance policyholder empathy with sound financial judgment.

## Professional Summary Examples

**For Entry-Level:**
"Detail-oriented insurance professional with a state adjuster license and 1 year of claims processing experience. Handled 40+ property and auto claims per month with a 97% documentation accuracy rate. Eager to apply strong analytical and customer service skills in a field adjuster role."

**For Mid-Level:**
"Licensed claims adjuster with 5 years of experience managing property, auto, and liability claims averaging $2.8M in total monthly reserves. Consistently closed 92% of claims within target cycle time while maintaining a customer satisfaction rating of 4.6/5. Experienced in subrogation recovery and SIU referrals."

**For Senior:**
"Senior claims adjuster with 10+ years of multi-line experience handling complex commercial and catastrophe claims. Managed a personal caseload of 150+ open claims with combined reserves exceeding $12M. Mentored a team of 6 junior adjusters, improving unit closure rate by 25% and reducing litigation referrals by 18%."

## Claims Adjuster Resume Format & Template Tips

Choosing the right resume format is critical for insurance roles:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for claims adjuster candidates with steady career progression
- **Professional resume format** — Use clean section headers, consistent spacing, and a modern but readable font
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Avoid graphics, tables, and columns that confuse applicant tracking systems
- **Resume length** — 1 page for entry-level / 1-2 pages for experienced / 2 pages for senior
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format across all devices and ATS platforms

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not listing your adjuster license** — State licensure is a baseline requirement; feature it prominently
2. **Omitting claim volume and reserves** — Hiring managers gauge your experience level by caseload size and complexity
3. **Ignoring software proficiency** — Xactimate, Guidewire, and other industry tools should be listed explicitly
4. **Being vague about outcomes** — Tie settlements, cycle times, and recovery amounts to specific numbers
5. **Forgetting customer service metrics** — Satisfaction scores and complaint ratios differentiate top adjusters
