## What Makes a Great Insurance Agent Resume?

This Insurance Agent resume example shows exactly what hiring managers want to see. The right resume format makes the difference between landing an interview and getting filtered out. Insurance agent resumes must balance sales performance with client service excellence. Agencies want to see your production numbers, retention rates, licensing credentials, and your ability to build a loyal book of business over time.

## Professional Summary Examples

**For Entry-Level:**
"Licensed Property & Casualty Insurance Agent with 1 year of experience in personal lines sales. Generated $350K in new premium during first year, earning Rookie of the Year recognition. Completed 200+ needs assessments and maintained a 95% customer satisfaction rating. Licensed in 3 states."

**For Mid-Level:**
"Accomplished Insurance Agent with 5+ years of experience in both personal and commercial lines. Manage a $2.5M book of business with a 91% renewal rate. Consistently exceeded monthly sales targets by 20%, writing an average of 30 new policies per month. Licensed in Property & Casualty, Life, and Health across 8 states."

**For Senior:**
"Top-producing Insurance Agent with 12+ years of experience and a $6M book of business across personal, commercial, and life insurance lines. Achieved Presidents Club status 7 consecutive years. Mentored and trained 15 new agents, with 10 reaching their first-year sales targets. Active community leader generating 40% of new business through referral networks."

## Essential Skills to Highlight

### Sales & Business Development
- New policy acquisition and quoting
- Cross-selling and multi-policy bundling
- Referral network development
- Cold calling and lead nurturing
- Sales presentations and consultative selling
- Annual revenue and premium growth

### Client Service & Retention
- Policy reviews and coverage recommendations
- Claims filing assistance and advocacy
- Client needs assessment and risk analysis
- Renewal management and retention strategies
- Customer relationship management
- Complaint resolution and service recovery

### Industry Knowledge & Compliance
- State licensing (P&C, Life, Health)
- Insurance regulations and compliance
- Underwriting guidelines and risk assessment
- Agency management systems (Applied Epic, Hawksoft, AMS360)
- Comparative rating platforms
- Continuing education and professional development

## Insurance Agent Resume Format & Template Tips

Choosing the right resume format is critical for insurance roles:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for Insurance Agent candidates with steady career progression in insurance or sales
- **Professional resume format** — Use clean section headers, consistent spacing, and a modern but readable font
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Avoid graphics, tables, and columns that confuse applicant tracking systems
- **Resume length** — 1 page for entry-level / 1-2 pages for experienced / 2 pages for senior
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format across all devices and ATS platforms

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not listing licenses and states** — Your licensing credentials are the first thing agencies and carriers check
2. **Omitting production numbers** — Premium written, policies sold, and book size are essential metrics for any insurance resume
3. **Ignoring retention metrics** — Retention rate and renewal percentage show you build lasting client relationships, not just chase new sales
4. **Forgetting technology skills** — Agency management systems and comparative raters are standard tools you must demonstrate familiarity with
5. **Being generic about insurance lines** — Specify whether you sell personal, commercial, life, health, or specialty lines
