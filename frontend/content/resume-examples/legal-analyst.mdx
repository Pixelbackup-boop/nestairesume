## What Makes a Great Legal Analyst Resume?

Legal analysts research and analyze legal issues to support decision-making. Analytical skills and legal knowledge are essential. With +5% growth and $72,000 average salary, Legal Analyst positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated Legal Analyst with training in Legal Research and Data Analysis. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Legal Analyst with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Legal Research and Report Writing with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

Legal Analyst professionals earn approximately **$72,000** with **+5%** projected growth.
