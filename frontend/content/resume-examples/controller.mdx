## What Makes a Great Controller Resume?

This controller resume example shows exactly what hiring managers want to see. The right resume format makes the difference between landing an interview and getting filtered out. A controller resume must convey strong financial stewardship, leadership ability, and a track record of ensuring accurate, timely reporting while implementing controls that protect the organization. CFOs and executive teams seek controllers who combine deep accounting expertise with strategic financial insight.

## Professional Summary Examples

**For Entry-Level:**
"CPA with 4 years of progressive accounting experience, including 2 years as Assistant Controller for a $30M manufacturing company. Managed month-end close processes, reducing close time from 12 to 7 business days. Supervised a team of 3 staff accountants and ensured full GAAP compliance across all financial statements."

**For Mid-Level:**
"Controller with 7 years of experience managing all accounting operations for a $75M multi-entity organization. Led a team of 8 accounting professionals, achieving clean audit opinions for 5 consecutive years. Implemented a new ERP system that automated 40% of manual journal entries and reduced reporting errors by 65%."

**For Senior:**
"Senior Controller with 12+ years of experience overseeing financial operations for organizations with revenues exceeding $200M. Managed consolidated financial reporting across 6 subsidiaries and 3 currencies. Reduced annual audit fees by $120,000 through improved internal controls and streamlined SOX compliance processes. Reported directly to the CFO and presented quarterly results to the board."

## Essential Skills to Highlight

### Financial Reporting & Compliance
- GAAP and IFRS financial reporting
- Month-end and year-end close management
- Consolidated financial statements
- Revenue recognition (ASC 606)
- Lease accounting (ASC 842)
- SOX compliance and internal controls

### Management & Operations
- Accounting team leadership and development
- Budget preparation and variance analysis
- Cash flow forecasting and treasury management
- Accounts payable and receivable oversight
- Tax planning and coordination
- External audit management

### Technology & Analysis
- ERP systems (SAP, NetSuite, Oracle, Sage)
- Advanced Excel and financial modeling
- Business intelligence tools (Power BI, Tableau)
- Accounting software (QuickBooks, Xero)
- Process automation and workflow optimization
- Data analytics and trend analysis

## Controller Resume Format & Template Tips

Choosing the right resume format is critical for finance roles:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for controller candidates with steady career progression
- **Professional resume format** — Use clean section headers, consistent spacing, and a modern but readable font
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Avoid graphics, tables, and columns that confuse applicant tracking systems
- **Resume length** — 1 page for entry-level / 1-2 pages for experienced / 2 pages for senior
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format across all devices and ATS platforms

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not listing CPA and professional credentials prominently** — CPA, CMA, and MBA credentials should appear near your name or in the header
2. **Omitting organizational revenue size** — The scale of the business you managed is the primary indicator of your experience level
3. **Ignoring audit outcomes** — Clean audit opinions and SOX compliance results are major proof points
4. **Being vague about close improvement** — Month-end close reduction is one of the most valued controller metrics; include specific timelines
5. **Forgetting ERP and technology experience** — Modern controllers must demonstrate technology proficiency; name your systems
