## What Makes a Great Housekeeping Supervisor Resume?

Housekeeping supervisors lead teams and ensure quality standards are met. The role balances people management with operational efficiency. With +6% growth and $38,000 average salary, Housekeeping Supervisor roles offer solid hospitality careers.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Enthusiastic Housekeeping Supervisor with training in Team Supervision and Quality Inspection. Strong commitment to guest satisfaction and team success."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Housekeeping Supervisor with 4+ years in hospitality. Proven record in Team Supervision and Scheduling with excellent guest feedback."

## Salary & Outlook

Housekeeping Supervisor professionals earn approximately **$38,000** with **+6%** growth projected.

## Common Mistakes

### Not quantifying team size

Include staff supervised and rooms under responsibility

### Missing quality metrics

Inspection scores, guest satisfaction, turnover rates



## Hiring Manager Tip

> **Housekeeping Supervisor resumes with guest satisfaction metrics and operational achievements get prioritized.**

"Housekeeping supervisor leading 20-person team in 400-room hotel, maintaining 95% quality inspection pass rate while reducing turnover 25%." Show team size, quality metrics, and leadership impact.

## Interview Questions

### "How do you handle call-offs with a full house?"

Discuss contingency planning and maintaining standards under pressure.

### "Describe your quality inspection process"

Cover standards, training, and addressing issues.
