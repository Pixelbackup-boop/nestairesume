## What Makes a Great Educational Technologist Resume?

An educational technologist resume must demonstrate both technical expertise and pedagogical understanding. Hiring administrators seek candidates who can implement technology solutions that enhance teaching and learning—not just manage systems.

Education hiring committees evaluate resumes based on instructional methodology, classroom management, and measurable student outcomes. Your resume should demonstrate not just where you have taught, but how your teaching approach has positively impacted student learning and engagement.

The most effective educational technologist resumes show platform implementation success, teacher adoption rates, and measurable impact on student engagement or outcomes through technology integration.

## Professional Summary Examples

### Entry-Level Educational Technologist
Former classroom teacher with instructional technology masters degree and Google Certified Educator credentials. Experienced in LMS administration, digital resource curation, and teacher technology coaching. Passionate about leveraging technology to personalize learning experiences.

### Mid-Level Educational Technologist
Educational Technologist with 4 years supporting 1:1 device program serving 2,500 students. Led Canvas LMS implementation achieving 95% teacher adoption. Expert in accessibility compliance, digital curriculum development, and professional development facilitation.

### Senior Educational Technologist
Director of Educational Technology leading district-wide digital transformation for 15 schools and 8,000 students. Managed $2M technology budget and 5-person team. Achieved 90% teacher proficiency in core platforms. Speaker at ISTE and state technology conferences.

## Achievement-Focused Bullet Points

Strong educational technologist bullet points quantify technology impact:

- Led LMS migration from Blackboard to Canvas serving 3,000 students with 97% teacher adoption
- Trained 150 teachers on digital tools achieving 85% integration in daily instruction
- Reduced help desk tickets by 40% through proactive training and resource development
- Implemented accessibility remediation ensuring 100% WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
- Developed digital curriculum repository with 500+ vetted resources used across 12 schools
- Managed $1.5M technology budget including device refresh and software licensing

## Educational Technologist Resume Format & Template Tips

1. **Lead with platform implementations**—show specific systems you deployed and adoption rates
2. **List technical certifications** (Google, Apple, LMS-specific) prominently
3. **Include teacher training volume** and proficiency outcomes
4. **Highlight accessibility and compliance experience**—increasingly important
5. **Show budget management** if applicable to demonstrate leadership

## Hiring Manager Tip

> **"I need someone who can get teachers actually using the technology, not just installing it. Show me your adoption rates and how you approach resistant teachers. Technical skills are assumed—change management is the hard part."**

School administrators hire educational technologists who combine technical ability with pedagogical understanding and change management skills. Your resume should demonstrate platform adoption rates, teacher training effectiveness, and ability to support instruction—not just manage devices.

## Common Educational Technologist Interview Questions

### 1. How do you approach training resistant teachers on new technology?
Describe your relationship-building, differentiated support, and strategies for demonstrating value to skeptical educators.

### 2. Walk me through a major technology implementation you led
Cover planning, communication, training, support systems, and how you measured success.

### 3. How do you evaluate new edtech tools before recommending adoption?
Discuss your evaluation criteria: pedagogical value, accessibility, privacy, integration, and cost.

### 4. How do you ensure equitable access to technology for all students?
Address device access, home connectivity, accessibility accommodations, and digital literacy gaps.

### 5. Describe your approach to data privacy and student information security
Show knowledge of FERPA, COPPA, and district data governance policies.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Technical skills only** without showing instructional impact
2. **No adoption rates or training metrics**—administrators need proof of effectiveness
3. **Missing certifications** (Google, Apple, LMS-specific)
4. **Generic IT experience** without education context
5. **Not demonstrating change management** and teacher support abilities
