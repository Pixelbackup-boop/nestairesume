## What Makes a Great Sales Manager Resume?

This sales manager resume example shows exactly what hiring managers want to see. The right resume format makes the difference between landing interviews and getting filtered out. An exceptional sales manager resume proves you can build, lead, and motivate teams to exceed revenue targets. Employers want hard numbers — quota attainment, revenue growth percentages, team performance, and deal sizes that demonstrate your direct impact on the bottom line.

## Professional Summary Examples

**For Entry-Level:**
"Results-driven sales team lead with 3 years of B2B sales experience and 1 year managing a team of 5 representatives. Exceeded personal quota by 130% for 8 consecutive quarters before promotion to team lead. Proficient in Salesforce, HubSpot, and data-driven pipeline management. A professional resume showcasing a strong track record of overachievement."

**For Mid-Level:**
"Sales Manager with 6 years of experience leading teams of 10-15 reps in SaaS and enterprise software sales. Grew territory revenue from $2M to $5.5M in 2 years while maintaining 95% team retention. Professional resume highlighting expertise in forecasting, territory planning, and coaching reps from onboarding to President's Club."

**For Senior:**
"VP of Sales with 12+ years building and scaling high-performance sales organizations from startup to $50M ARR. Recruited, trained, and managed 60+ sales professionals across 4 regional teams. Consistently achieved 115%+ of annual revenue targets while reducing customer acquisition costs by 25%."

## Sales Manager Resume Format & Template Tips

Choosing the right resume format is critical for sales leadership roles:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for sales manager candidates with steady career progression, listing your most recent sales leadership experience first to show upward trajectory
- **Professional resume format** — Use clean section headers, consistent spacing, and a modern but readable font. Include a "Key Metrics" or "Revenue Highlights" section near the top
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Avoid graphics, tables, and columns that confuse applicant tracking systems. Stick to standard section headers
- **Resume length** — 1-2 pages for mid-level sales managers, 2 pages for VP-level leaders with extensive organizational impact
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format across all devices and ATS platforms
- Lead every bullet point with a revenue metric or team performance number to immediately demonstrate your value

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **No revenue numbers** — A sales manager resume without dollar figures and percentages is incomplete
2. **Ignoring team metrics** — Include team size, retention rates, and team quota attainment alongside personal numbers
3. **Vague leadership claims** — Replace "managed sales team" with "led 12-person sales team exceeding $8M annual quota by 125%"
4. **Missing CRM expertise** — Salesforce and HubSpot proficiency are table stakes for modern sales management
5. **Overlooking coaching impact** — Highlight how your mentoring directly improved individual rep performance and promotions

## ATS Optimization for Sales Manager Resumes

Sales organizations use applicant tracking systems to filter candidates efficiently. To pass ATS screening with an ats resume format:
- Include keywords like "revenue growth," "quota attainment," "pipeline management," "CRM," "Salesforce," and "team leadership" directly from the job posting
- Use an ats resume template with standard section headers — avoid creative layouts
- Spell out abbreviations at least once (e.g., "Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)")
- List specific sales methodologies mentioned in the job description (e.g., "MEDDIC," "Challenger Sale")
- Include industry-specific terms like "SaaS," "B2B," "enterprise sales," "mid-market," or "SMB" to match the job level
- Mirror the exact language from the job description to maximize your ATS match score
