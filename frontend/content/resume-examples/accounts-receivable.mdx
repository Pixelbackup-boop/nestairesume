## What Makes a Great Accounts Receivable Specialist Resume?

This accounts receivable specialist resume example illustrates how to present your collections performance and financial accuracy in a way that controllers and finance managers value. The right resume format puts your DSO improvements, collection rates, and ERP proficiency front and center. A strong accounts receivable resume demonstrates that you can manage high volumes of invoices, resolve disputes efficiently, and keep cash flowing. Your resume example should prove your attention to detail and reliability with numbers. With the proper resume format, hiring managers see a trustworthy financial professional who protects the bottom line.

## Accounts Receivable Resume Format & Template Tips

Choosing the right resume format is essential for finance roles:
- **Chronological resume format** — Best for AR specialists with consistent finance career progression, showing growth in responsibility and portfolio size
- **Combination resume format** — This resume format works well if transitioning from customer service or bookkeeping into a dedicated AR role
- **ATS-friendly resume template** — Use standard section headers and list ERP systems, software, and financial certifications as keywords
- **Resume length** — 1 page for most candidates. Your resume format should prioritize metrics like collection rates, DSO, and portfolio size
- Save your resume as PDF to preserve your resume format and ensure tables and numbers display correctly

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **No financial metrics** — Collection rates, DSO improvements, and portfolio size must be included with specific numbers
2. **Listing software without proficiency level** — Specify whether you are proficient, advanced, or expert in each ERP system
3. **Ignoring soft skills** — Customer communication, negotiation, and problem resolution are critical for collections
4. **Vague job descriptions** — "Handled accounts receivable" says nothing; quantify volume, accuracy, and outcomes
5. **Omitting process improvements** — If you automated or streamlined AR processes, that achievement deserves a prominent bullet

## ATS Optimization for Accounts Receivable Resumes

Finance departments use Applicant Tracking Systems to filter candidates efficiently. To ensure your ats resume gets through:
- Use standard section headers (Experience, Skills, Education, Certifications) in your ats resume format
- Include exact keywords from the job posting such as "accounts receivable," "collections," "account reconciliation," "SAP," and "aging reports"
- Use an ats resume template that avoids complex formatting, graphics, or non-standard layouts
- Spell out software names fully and include common abbreviations (e.g., "Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)")
