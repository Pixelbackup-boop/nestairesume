## What Makes a Great Florist Resume?

Florists design and create floral arrangements for events and everyday occasions. With -5% growth and $32,000 average salary, Florist positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated Florist with training in Floral Design and Customer Service. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled Florist with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Floral Design and Plant Care with excellent results."
