## What Makes a Great TSA Agent Resume?

TSA agents screen passengers and baggage to ensure aviation security. With +2% growth and $45,000 average salary, TSA Agent positions offer solid career opportunities.

## Professional Summary Examples

**Entry-Level:** "Motivated TSA Agent with training in Security Screening and Customer Service. Strong work ethic with commitment to excellence."

**Experienced:** "Skilled TSA Agent with 4+ years of experience. Proven track record in Security Screening and Attention to Detail with excellent results."

## Salary & Job Outlook

TSA Agent professionals earn approximately **$45,000** with **+2%** projected growth.
