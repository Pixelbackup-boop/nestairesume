## 英文求职信怎么写：外企Cover Letter完全指南

申请外企、海外岗位或国际公司时，英文求职信（Cover Letter）几乎是必备材料。一封专业的Cover Letter不仅展示你的英语能力，更展示你的职业素养和沟通技巧。

### Cover Letter的标准结构

**Header（信头）：**
包含你的联系信息、日期、收件人信息。

**Opening Paragraph（开头段）：**
2-3句话，说明申请岗位和核心卖点。

**Body Paragraphs（正文段，1-2段）：**
用具体事例和数据证明你的能力和匹配度。

**Closing Paragraph（结尾段）：**
表达面试意愿，感谢对方的时间。

### Cover Letter范文

> Dear Ms. Chen,
>
> I am writing to express my strong interest in the Senior Product Manager position at [Company]. With 8 years of experience leading cross-functional product teams and a proven track record of launching products that generated over ¥100M in annual revenue, I am confident in my ability to drive your product strategy forward.
>
> At my current role with [Current Company], I led the redesign of our flagship mobile app, resulting in a 45% increase in daily active users and a 30% improvement in user retention over 12 months. I also established a data-driven product development framework that reduced time-to-market by 25% while maintaining a 4.8-star app store rating.
>
> [Company]'s commitment to innovation in the fintech space deeply resonates with my professional passion. I am particularly impressed by your recent launch of [Product], and I believe my experience in scaling digital products in the Chinese market would be a valuable asset to your team.
>
> I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my experience aligns with your team's goals. Thank you for your time and consideration.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your Name]

### 常用英文句型

**开头句型：**
- "I am writing to apply for the [Position] role at [Company]..."
- "With X years of experience in [Field], I am excited to apply for..."
- "Your posting for [Position] immediately caught my attention because..."

**正文句型：**
- "In my current role at [Company], I have..."
- "A key achievement was..."
- "This experience has equipped me with..."

**结尾句型：**
- "I would welcome the opportunity to discuss..."
- "I am eager to bring my skills to [Company]..."
- "Thank you for considering my application."

### 中国求职者常见英文错误

| 错误表达 | 正确表达 |
|----------|----------|
| "I am a hard-working person" | "I consistently exceed targets by..." |
| "I can do everything" | "My core strengths include..." |
| "Please give me a chance" | "I would welcome the opportunity to discuss..." |
| "I graduated from XX university" | 放在简历中，Cover Letter聚焦成果 |

### 不同行业Cover Letter侧重点

- **科技行业** — 技术栈、项目影响力、创新案例
- **金融行业** — 合规意识、量化成果、专业认证
- **咨询行业** — 分析能力、客户案例、团队协作
- **市场营销** — 创意案例、数据驱动、品牌影响
