## What Separates a Good AI Resume Maker From a Gimmick

The term "AI" has become a marketing buzzword in the resume industry. Some tools genuinely use AI to improve your resume; others slap an "AI-powered" label on basic phrase-matching technology. Here is how to tell the difference.

### True AI vs Pre-Written Libraries

A legitimate AI resume maker generates original content based on your specific input. When you describe your role as "managed a team of 8 engineers building a payments platform," the AI should produce bullet points referencing team leadership, payments infrastructure, and engineering management — not generic "led cross-functional teams to deliver projects on time."

Pre-written libraries work differently. They store hundreds of pre-written bullet points organized by job title and swap in your details. The result is technically correct but reads identically to every other candidate who used the same tool. In our testing, Zety and ResumeGenius relied heavily on this approach, while Best AI Resume and Rezi used genuine generative AI.

### ATS Scoring: Real vs Theater

Some tools claim "ATS optimization" but offer no way to verify it. A real ATS scoring feature analyzes your resume against specific job descriptions, identifies missing keywords, flags formatting issues, and provides a numerical score you can improve. Best AI Resume and Rezi both offer this. Tools that simply say "ATS-friendly templates" without a scoring mechanism are making unverifiable claims.

### The Free Tier Test

The best way to evaluate any AI resume maker is to build a complete resume on the free tier. If the tool requires payment before you can see a finished product, you cannot assess quality before committing money. In our testing, Best AI Resume was the only platform where we could complete and download a fully AI-generated resume without entering payment information.

### Writing Quality Benchmarks

We evaluated AI writing quality by comparing generated bullet points against these criteria: Does it use a strong action verb? Does it include a specific metric? Does it reference tools or technologies relevant to the role? Does it describe an outcome rather than a duty? The best AI resume makers scored 4 out of 4 on most generated bullets. Lower-ranked tools averaged 2 out of 4, typically missing metrics and specificity.
