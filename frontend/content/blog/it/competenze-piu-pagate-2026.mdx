### Come Sviluppare Queste Competenze

1. **Certificazioni** — AWS, Google Cloud, PMP, CISSP
2. **Corsi online** — Coursera, edX, Udacity, LinkedIn Learning
3. **Master specialistici** — MIP, SDA Bocconi, Polimi
4. **Progetti pratici** — Portfolio GitHub, contributi open source
5. **Networking** — Community professionali, meetup, conferenze
