**Biodata** — short for biographical data — is a document format widely used in South Asia, the Middle East, and parts of Africa for both job applications and marriage proposals. If you've been asked to submit a biodata and aren't sure what to include, or you're looking for a **biodata format example with answers**, this guide covers everything.

## What Is Biodata Format and When Do You Need It?

A biodata is a structured document that combines personal information with professional qualifications. The term is especially common in [South Asian and Middle Eastern job markets](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/), where employers expect a different format than the Western-style resume. Unlike a resume (which focuses solely on career qualifications), a biodata includes:

- **Personal details:** Name, date of birth, gender, nationality, marital status
- **Family background:** Parents' names, occupations, siblings (for marriage biodata)
- **Educational qualifications:** Degrees, institutions, grades
- **Professional experience:** Work history, skills, achievements
- **Additional information:** Hobbies, languages, references

### Two types of biodata:

1. **Professional biodata (for jobs)** — Emphasizes education, work experience, and skills. Personal details are minimal and job-relevant.
2. **Marriage biodata** — Emphasizes personal characteristics, family background, and values. Professional details are secondary.

This guide covers both with templates you can use immediately.

## Biodata vs Resume vs CV

| Feature | Biodata | Resume | CV (Curriculum Vitae) |
|---------|---------|--------|----------------------|
| **Personal details** | Extensive (DOB, religion, family) | Minimal (name, contact only) | Minimal |
| **Focus** | Personal + professional | Professional achievements | Academic & research |
| **Length** | 1-2 pages | 1-2 pages | 2-10+ pages |
| **Photo** | Usually included | Rarely (country-dependent) | Sometimes (academia) |
| **Used in** | South Asia, Middle East, Africa | North America, Europe, Australia | Academia worldwide |
| **Customized per job?** | Rarely | Always | Sometimes |

For more on how resumes differ across industries, see the [Glassdoor Career Guide](https://www.glassdoor.com/blog/guide/resume-tips/).

## Tips for Writing a Better Biodata

### For job biodata:
- **Focus on qualifications over personal details** — Modern employers care about skills and experience, not religion or family background
- **Include metrics** — Same as a resume, quantify your achievements where possible
- **Keep it to 1-2 pages** — Don't list every detail; curate for relevance
- **Use a professional photo** — If including a photo, use a passport-style headshot with neutral background
- **Match the format to the employer** — MNCs and tech companies prefer resumes; traditional companies and government jobs may prefer biodata

### For marriage biodata:
- **Be honest** — Misrepresenting details creates problems later
- **Keep it respectful and positive** — Avoid listing rigid "requirements" for a partner
- **Use a recent, clear photo** — Natural lighting, appropriate attire
- **Proofread carefully** — Spelling errors in a biodata create a poor first impression

## When to Use Biodata vs Resume

| Situation | Use Biodata | Use Resume |
|-----------|-------------|------------|
| Indian government job | Yes | No |
| Indian private sector (traditional) | Yes | Sometimes |
| MNC in India | No | Yes |
| International job application | No | Yes |
| Marriage proposal | Yes | No |
| Visa/immigration | No | Yes (+ experience letters) |
| Freelance/contract work | No | Yes |

## Convert Your Biodata to a Modern Resume

If you currently have a biodata and want to create a resume for international or modern applications:

1. **Remove personal details** — Date of birth, religion, marital status, family details
2. **Add a professional summary** — 2-3 lines highlighting your strongest qualifications
3. **Rewrite experience as achievements** — Transform duty descriptions into result-oriented bullet points
4. **Remove the photo** — Unless applying in a region where photos are customary
5. **Add keywords** — Include industry-specific terms for ATS compatibility
