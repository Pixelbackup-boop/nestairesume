## Contoh Surat Iringan Terbaik untuk Pasaran Kerja Malaysia 2026

Melihat contoh surat iringan yang berjaya memberikan anda gambaran jelas tentang apa yang dicari oleh majikan. Artikel ini menyediakan contoh surat iringan terbaik untuk pelbagai jawatan dan peringkat kerjaya di Malaysia.

### Contoh Surat Iringan: Graduan Baru (Bahasa Inggeris)

> Dear Hiring Manager,
>
> I am writing to apply for the Junior Software Developer position at Grab Malaysia. As a recent Computer Science graduate from Universiti Malaya with a CGPA of 3.7, I am eager to contribute to Grab's mission of driving Southeast Asia forward.
>
> During my final year project, I developed a ride-sharing optimization algorithm using Python and machine learning that reduced estimated wait times by 18%. My 6-month internship at a fintech startup gave me hands-on experience with React, Node.js, and AWS services, where I contributed to 3 production features used by over 10,000 daily users.
>
> I am particularly drawn to Grab's engineering culture of innovation and your commitment to solving real-world transportation challenges. I believe my combination of academic excellence and practical development experience makes me a strong fit for this role.
>
> I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I can contribute to your engineering team. Thank you for your consideration.
>
> Best regards,
> Ahmad Razif

### Contoh Surat Iringan: Profesional Berpengalaman (Bahasa Malaysia)

> Yang Dihormati Puan Siti Aminah,
>
> Saya menulis untuk memohon jawatan Pengurus Kewangan Kanan di Petronas seperti yang diiklankan di LinkedIn. Dengan pengalaman 12 tahun dalam pengurusan kewangan korporat dan kelayakan CPA Malaysia, saya yakin dapat menyumbang secara bermakna kepada pasukan kewangan Petronas.
>
> Sebagai Pengurus Kewangan di Sime Darby selama 5 tahun terakhir, saya telah menguruskan belanjawan operasi RM200 juta dan memimpin pasukan 20 orang. Pencapaian utama saya termasuk mengurangkan kos operasi sebanyak 15% melalui penstrukturan semula proses kewangan dan memperkenalkan sistem pelaporan automatik yang menjimatkan 100+ jam kerja sebulan.
>
> Komitmen Petronas terhadap kecemerlangan kewangan dan transformasi digital sangat sejajar dengan kemahiran saya dalam SAP S/4HANA, analisis data, dan pengurusan risiko. Saya berharap dapat membincangkan bagaimana pengalaman saya boleh membantu mencapai matlamat kewangan Petronas.
>
> Terima kasih atas pertimbangan anda.
>
> Yang benar,
> Mohammad Hafiz

### Contoh Surat Iringan: Penukar Kerjaya

> Dear Ms. Lee,
>
> I am excited to apply for the Digital Marketing Specialist role at AirAsia. After 5 years in traditional sales, I have completed a Professional Certificate in Digital Marketing from Google and am ready to apply my customer relationship skills to the digital landscape.
>
> My sales background gives me unique insight into customer behavior — I consistently exceeded quarterly targets by 25% through data-driven approaches. I have since built a personal blog that attracts 15,000 monthly visitors through SEO strategies I developed independently, demonstrating my ability to drive organic growth.
>
> AirAsia's innovative approach to digital marketing, particularly the Super App ecosystem, aligns perfectly with my passion for technology-driven customer engagement. I am confident that my combination of sales expertise and digital marketing skills can contribute to your team's growth objectives.
>
> I look forward to discussing this opportunity further.
>
> Warm regards,
> Priya Nair

### Panduan Menggunakan Contoh Surat Iringan

1. **Perhatikan struktur** — Bagaimana setiap contoh mengalir dari pengenalan ke penutup
2. **Perhatikan angka** — Setiap contoh menyertakan pencapaian terukur
3. **Perhatikan penyelidikan** — Setiap contoh menyebut syarikat secara spesifik
4. **Sesuaikan** — Jangan salin; gunakan sebagai inspirasi untuk versi anda sendiri
