## What Makes an Occupational Therapist Cover Letter Stand Out?

A compelling occupational therapist cover letter demonstrates your ability to help patients regain independence in daily activities through evidence-based interventions. Unlike general healthcare roles, OT positions require showing you understand functional assessment, can develop individualized treatment plans, and measure meaningful outcomes that improve patients' quality of life.

Your cover letter should prove you can restore function and independence through skilled therapeutic intervention.

## Occupational Therapist Cover Letter Example

Here's a proven cover letter format for occupational therapist positions:

**Example for Occupational Therapist:**

---

Dear [Hiring Manager/Rehab Director],

I am writing to apply for the Occupational Therapist position at [Facility Name]. Your commitment to [specific aspect — patient-centered care, innovative rehabilitation, community integration] aligns with my approach to occupational therapy practice. With 6+ years of experience helping patients achieve functional independence, I'm confident I can contribute to your rehabilitation team's success.

At [Current Facility], I provide occupational therapy services to a diverse caseload of 45+ patients weekly across acute care and outpatient settings. My patients consistently achieve positive outcomes — 85% meet their ADL goals within projected timeframes, and my discharge-to-home rate for stroke patients exceeds the department average by 15%. I specialize in upper extremity rehabilitation and have developed a hand therapy program that reduced average treatment duration by 3 weeks while maintaining outcome quality.

My clinical approach combines evidence-based practice with individualized care. I conduct thorough assessments using standardized tools (FIM, COPM, DASH) and develop treatment plans addressing patients' meaningful activities and life roles. I'm skilled in adaptive equipment prescription, splint fabrication, and environmental modification. I collaborate closely with physicians, PTs, speech therapists, and case managers to ensure coordinated care that addresses the whole patient.

I'm licensed in [State] with NBCOT certification and pursuing Certified Hand Therapist (CHT) credentials. I stay current through continuing education and have presented on constraint-induced movement therapy at our regional OT conference. I'm passionate about helping patients return to the activities that matter most to them.

I'm drawn to [Facility Name]'s [specific aspect — patient population, rehabilitation philosophy, growth opportunities]. My experience in [relevant setting or population] positions me to contribute immediately to your team.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my occupational therapy experience aligns with your needs. Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

---

## Key Elements of an Effective Occupational Therapist Cover Letter

### 1. Licensure
"Licensed in [State] with NBCOT certification" establishes legal practice credentials.

### 2. Caseload Management
"45+ patients weekly" demonstrates productivity capacity.

### 3. Outcome Metrics
"85% meet ADL goals" proves treatment effectiveness.

### 4. Clinical Specialization
Hand therapy program development shows advanced skills.

### 5. Interdisciplinary Collaboration
Working with physicians, PTs, and case managers shows team integration.

## Cover Letters by Occupational Therapy Specialization

### Pediatric Occupational Therapist
- Emphasize child development and sensory integration
- Mention school-based and early intervention experience
- Highlight family-centered care and play-based therapy

### Hand Therapist
- Focus on upper extremity rehabilitation and splinting
- Mention CHT certification or candidacy
- Highlight post-surgical and trauma experience

### Acute Care/Hospital OT
- Emphasize medical complexity and rapid assessment
- Mention discharge planning and equipment provision
- Highlight collaboration with medical teams

### Outpatient/Orthopedic OT
- Focus on return-to-function and work rehabilitation
- Mention progressive treatment protocols
- Highlight outcome measurement and documentation

### Mental Health OT
- Emphasize life skills and community integration
- Mention psychosocial interventions and groups
- Highlight recovery-oriented practice

## Metrics to Include in Your Occupational Therapist Cover Letter

**Always include:**
- License and certification status
- Caseload size and settings
- Years of OT experience
- Specialty areas

**Powerful additions:**
- Goal achievement rates
- Discharge outcomes
- Patient satisfaction scores
- Program development
- Continuing education or presentations

## Common Occupational Therapist Cover Letter Mistakes

- **No license mention** — OT licensure is required; state it clearly
- **Vague outcome claims** — Functional improvements should be measurable
- **Generic rehabilitation focus** — OT's unique value is occupation-based; emphasize it
- **Missing setting context** — Practice settings matter; specify your experience
- **Overlooking collaboration** — OT works in teams; demonstrate interprofessional skills
- **No patient-centered language** — Meaningful activities and life roles define OT; reference them
