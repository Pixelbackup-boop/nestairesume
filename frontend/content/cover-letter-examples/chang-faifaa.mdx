## Thai Electrician Cover Letter Formatting & Structure Tips

For a Thai electrician (ช่างไฟฟ้า) role, licensing is the first thing a project engineer or maintenance supervisor looks for, so lead with it. Open by stating your Department of Skill Development (กรมพัฒนาฝีมือแรงงาน) electrical license grade, your Provincial Electricity Authority (PEA) or Metropolitan Electricity Authority (MEA) registration where relevant, and your voltage class — low voltage, or qualified for medium/high voltage work. A line like "licensed ช่างไฟฟ้าภายในอาคาร, grade 1, cleared for 380V three-phase industrial systems" tells the reader you can be put on a live job. Keep the letter to one page in a clean 10-12pt font, single column, with no graphics that scanning software cannot read.

Build the body around what electrical hiring managers actually verify: electrical installation and wiring to the Thai EIT/wiring standard, PLC programming and panel work, blueprint and single-line diagram reading, and fault troubleshooting with measured downtime reduction. Quantify in trade terms — circuits installed, panels commissioned, mean time to repair, and your safety record (lost-time incidents, lockout/tagout compliance, hours worked injury-free). Name the equipment and brands you know (Schneider, Mitsubishi, Siemens PLCs) since supervisors screen for hands-on familiarity. Close by confirming your willingness to work shifts, shutdowns, and site postings, and your strict adherence to electrical safety standards.

## ATS Optimization

Many employers use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to screen cover letters before a human reads them. To pass these filters, mirror keywords from the job description naturally throughout your letter. Avoid using headers in text boxes, tables, or graphics that ATS software cannot parse. Save your file as a PDF unless the employer specifically requests a different format.

Include the exact job title and key qualifications mentioned in the posting. If the role requires "project management experience," use that exact phrase rather than a synonym. This ensures your application scores well in automated screening while still reading naturally to hiring managers.
