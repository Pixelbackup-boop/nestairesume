## Thai Teacher Cover Letter

Professional Thai Teacher cover letter example with industry-specific tips.

This khruu cover letter guide provides actionable tips and expert recommendations to help you stand out.


## Thai Teacher Cover Letter Formatting & Structure Tips

School principals and academic directors read teaching cover letters fast, so put your teaching license and grade-level specialty in the first two lines of the body — state your Thai teaching license number (or กว.), whether you teach ภาษาไทย at the primary or secondary level, and your years in front of a classroom. Keep it to one page on plain white with a 10.5–12pt serif or Calibri font and 1-inch margins, because letters that mirror a clean lesson handout signal classroom organization. Name the specific school and reference its program (bilingual, IEP/EP, or Thai-medium government track) in your opening line so it never reads as a mass mailing.

Build the body around evidence a hiring committee can verify: classroom management approach, measurable student gains (O-NET score improvements, reading-fluency growth, or pass rates), and how you align lessons to OBEC standards and the Basic Education Core Curriculum. Devote one paragraph to your teaching philosophy and one to a concrete result — for example, "raised Prathom 5 Thai reading-comprehension scores by 18% over one academic year using leveled reading circles." Close by inviting a demonstration lesson or interview, since most Thai schools require a teaching demo before any offer.

## ATS Optimization

Many employers use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to screen cover letters before a human reads them. To pass these filters, mirror keywords from the job description naturally throughout your letter. Avoid using headers in text boxes, tables, or graphics that ATS software cannot parse. Save your file as a PDF unless the employer specifically requests a different format.

Include the exact job title and key qualifications mentioned in the posting. If the role requires "project management experience," use that exact phrase rather than a synonym. This ensures your application scores well in automated screening while still reading naturally to hiring managers.
