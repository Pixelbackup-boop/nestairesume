## What Makes a Physical Therapist Cover Letter Stand Out?

A compelling physical therapist cover letter demonstrates your clinical expertise, patient outcome achievements, and rehabilitation philosophy. Unlike medical roles focused on diagnosis, PT positions require showing you can assess movement dysfunction, develop effective treatment plans, and motivate patients through recovery.

Your cover letter should prove you combine evidence-based clinical skills with the interpersonal abilities needed to help patients achieve their functional goals.

## Physical Therapist Cover Letter Example

Here's a proven cover letter format for physical therapist positions:

**Example for Physical Therapist:**

---

Dear [Hiring Manager's Name],

I am writing to apply for the Physical Therapist position at [Facility/Practice Name]. Your organization's commitment to [specific approach — patient outcomes, specialized population, innovative techniques] aligns with my passion for helping patients restore function and return to the activities they love. As a licensed Physical Therapist with 5+ years of outpatient orthopedic experience, I'm excited about the opportunity to contribute to your rehabilitation team.

At [Current Facility], I manage a caseload of 50-60 patients weekly across diverse orthopedic conditions including post-surgical rehabilitation, sports injuries, and chronic pain. My evidence-based approach has produced strong outcomes — 89% of my patients meet or exceed their functional goals within projected timeframes, and my patient satisfaction scores average 4.9/5.0. I specialize in manual therapy techniques combined with progressive therapeutic exercise, achieving excellent results with post-operative knee and shoulder patients.

I'm committed to staying current with emerging evidence. I regularly participate in continuing education, recently completing advanced training in dry needling and instrument-assisted soft tissue mobilization. I'm pursuing my Orthopedic Clinical Specialist (OCS) certification and expect to complete board examination within the next 6 months.

I'm particularly drawn to [Facility Name]'s [specific aspect — patient population, treatment philosophy, professional development opportunities]. My experience with [relevant specialty or population] and my commitment to outcome-driven care position me to contribute immediately to your team.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my clinical expertise and patient-centered approach align with your facility's needs. Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely,
[Your Name], PT, DPT

---

## Key Elements of an Effective Physical Therapist Cover Letter

### 1. Licensure and Credentials
"Licensed Physical Therapist" with degree credentials (PT, DPT) establishes authority.

### 2. Caseload Context
"50-60 patients weekly" demonstrates experience managing substantial patient volumes.

### 3. Outcome Metrics
"89% meet or exceed functional goals" quantifies treatment effectiveness.

### 4. Treatment Approach
Mentioning manual therapy and progressive exercise shows clinical philosophy.

### 5. Professional Development
Continuing education and specialty certification pursuit demonstrate commitment to growth.

## Cover Letters by Physical Therapy Setting

### Outpatient Orthopedic PT
- Emphasize musculoskeletal expertise
- Mention post-surgical and sports injury experience
- Highlight manual therapy and exercise prescription

### Acute Care/Hospital PT
- Focus on early mobility and discharge planning
- Mention ICU, cardiac, and surgical populations
- Highlight interdisciplinary collaboration

### Pediatric PT
- Emphasize developmental expertise
- Mention early intervention and school-based experience
- Highlight family-centered care approach

### Neurological PT
- Focus on stroke, TBI, and spinal cord injury
- Mention neuroplasticity-based interventions
- Highlight functional independence outcomes

### Home Health PT
- Emphasize independence and safety focus
- Mention caregiver education and home modification
- Highlight autonomous clinical decision-making

## Metrics to Include in Your Physical Therapist Cover Letter

**Always include:**
- Caseload volume
- Patient outcome percentages (goal achievement)
- Patient satisfaction scores
- Discharge rates and timelines

**Powerful additions:**
- Productivity metrics (balanced with quality)
- Specific population outcomes
- Continuing education investments
- Mentorship or student supervision
- Program development contributions

## Common Physical Therapist Cover Letter Mistakes

- **No outcome data** — Physical therapy effectiveness should be measurable; quantify your results
- **Productivity-only focus** — Balance efficiency metrics with quality and outcomes
- **Generic rehabilitation interest** — Orthopedic vs. neuro vs. pediatric require different skills
- **Missing specialty interests** — Highlight your specific clinical interests and expertise
- **Overlooking soft skills** — Patient motivation and education are core PT competencies
- **No continuing education mention** — PT requires lifelong learning; show your commitment
