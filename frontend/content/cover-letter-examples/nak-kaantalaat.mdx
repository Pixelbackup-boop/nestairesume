## Formatting & Structure Tips

For a Thai marketing professional (nak-kaantalaat), hiring managers at Thai brands and agencies want proof you understand the local platform mix, not a generic "global digital marketer." Lead the header with the channels that actually move Thai consumers — LINE Official Account, Facebook, TikTok, and increasingly Shopee/Lazada ads — and if you're targeting an English-speaking multinational in Bangkok, keep the letter in clean professional English while signaling native Thai fluency, since bilingual copy and community management is half the job. One page, simple font, no clutter; Thai agency reviewers move fast through stacks of applicants.

Build the body around campaign numbers in the metrics Thai marketers report: LINE OA follower growth and broadcast open rates, Facebook/TikTok ad ROAS and CPM in baht, TikTok view-through and engagement rate, and conversion or GMV lift on marketplace campaigns. Name the tools by their Thai-market reality — LINE OA Manager, Meta Business Suite, TikTok Ads Manager, and SEO for Thai-language search intent. If you've run influencer and KOL collaborations (a core channel here) or seasonal pushes around Songkran, 11.11, and end-of-year sales, say so with results attached. Tie each selling point to a measurable outcome (for example, "grew LINE OA to 80K followers and lifted broadcast CTR to 6%") rather than listing duties, and let your understanding of Thai consumer behavior come through as your real differentiator.

## ATS Optimization

Many employers use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to screen cover letters before a human reads them. To pass these filters, mirror keywords from the job description naturally throughout your letter. Avoid using headers in text boxes, tables, or graphics that ATS software cannot parse. Save your file as a PDF unless the employer specifically requests a different format.

Include the exact job title and key qualifications mentioned in the posting. If the role requires "project management experience," use that exact phrase rather than a synonym. This ensures your application scores well in automated screening while still reading naturally to hiring managers.
