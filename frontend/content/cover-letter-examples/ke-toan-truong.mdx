## Formatting & Structure Tips

A chief accountant cover letter is read by a CFO or finance director who is, above all, assessing whether they can trust you with statutory compliance and the close. Open by stating your professional standing in the first line — your accounting credential (CPA, ACCA, or a local chief-accountant certificate / chứng chỉ kế toán trưởng) and the years you've led a finance function — because the law in many jurisdictions requires a qualified, certified chief accountant to sign the books. Keep it to one page, formal in tone, and put your certification in the header where it registers before the body.

Build the body around the three things that define the role: reporting accuracy, tax and regulatory compliance, and the integrity of the close. One paragraph should demonstrate command of the standards you report under — IFRS or VAS (Vietnamese Accounting Standards) — and the ERP you run the ledger in (SAP, Oracle, Misa, Fast, or Bravo). A second should prove control and compliance with hard outcomes: "delivered a clean external audit with zero adjustments three years running," "shortened the monthly close from 12 to 6 days," or "passed a tax inspection with no penalties." Avoid creative formatting; finance leaders read a chief accountant's letter as a sample of how disciplined and precise their reporting will be.

## ATS Optimization

Finance teams and recruitment agencies filter chief accountant applications through ATS software, so mirror the posting's exact compliance and systems vocabulary. If the role specifies "VAS," "IFRS," "tax finalization," or a named ERP like "SAP" or "Misa," use those literal terms rather than "accounting standards" or "ERP system." Seed the document with the keywords these listings consistently screen for: financial reporting, tax compliance, audit management, internal controls, budgeting, consolidation, statutory reporting, and month-end close.

Match the exact job title posted — this role appears as "Chief Accountant," "Kế toán trưởng," "Head of Accounting," and "Accounting Manager," and the title-match score depends on using theirs. Name your certification precisely as the ad requests (CPA, ACCA, chief-accountant certificate) so the qualification filter clears. Keep figures and standards in plain text — no tables or columns, which ATS parsers often drop — and submit as a PDF unless the employer asks for .docx.
