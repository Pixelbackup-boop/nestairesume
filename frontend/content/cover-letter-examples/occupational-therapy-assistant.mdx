## What Makes an Occupational Therapy Assistant Cover Letter Stand Out?

Occupational therapy assistants implement treatment plans that help patients regain independence in daily activities. Hiring managers look for candidates with strong intervention skills, patient rapport, and the ability to work effectively under OT supervision. Your cover letter should demonstrate treatment competence, patient engagement ability, and the collaborative approach that makes the OT/OTA team effective.

The best occupational therapy assistant cover letters show evidence of patient progress, population experience, and the motivational skills that encourage patient participation.

## Occupational Therapy Assistant Cover Letter Example

Here's a cover letter that demonstrates OTA excellence:

**Example for Experienced OTA:**

---

Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm applying for the Occupational Therapy Assistant position at [Facility Name]. Your facility's commitment to patient-centered rehabilitation aligns with my passion for helping patients regain independence. As a certified occupational therapy assistant with 4 years of experience helping patients achieve meaningful functional goals, I'm excited about the opportunity to join your therapy team.

At [Current Facility], I implement occupational therapy interventions across diverse patient populations. Key accomplishments include:

- Implement treatment plans for 12+ patients daily across skilled nursing and outpatient settings, with 88% achieving functional goals within projected timeframes
- Specialize in ADL retraining and adaptive equipment training, helping patients regain independence in bathing, dressing, and meal preparation
- Fabricate custom splints and adaptive devices that improve patient function and comfort, receiving recognition for creative problem-solving
- Maintain excellent working relationship with supervising OTs, providing detailed progress reports and observations that inform treatment modifications

What distinguishes my approach is understanding that occupational therapy is about meaningful activity — not just exercise. When I work with a stroke patient on fine motor skills, I don't just hand them pegs. I learn what matters to them — maybe it's holding grandchildren, returning to woodworking, or managing their own medications. Then I design activities around those goals. Patients engage more fully when therapy connects to their real lives. That meaning-centered approach accelerates progress and improves outcomes.

I hold COTA certification with current state licensure and BLS. I'm experienced in skilled nursing, outpatient, and home health settings. I'd welcome the opportunity to bring my OTA skills to your rehabilitation team.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

---

## Key Elements That Make This Cover Letter Effective

### 1. Patient Volume and Outcomes
12+ daily with 88% goal achievement demonstrates productivity and effectiveness.

### 2. ADL Specialty
Independence in meaningful activities shows core OT focus.

### 3. Problem-Solving
Splint fabrication with recognition demonstrates clinical creativity.

### 4. OT Collaboration
Progress reports informing modifications shows appropriate role.

### 5. Meaning-Centered Philosophy
"Meaningful activity — not just exercise" articulates OT values.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Missing outcome metrics** — Patient progress is measurable; include rates
- **Ignoring OT relationship** — Collaboration is essential; address it clearly
- **Vague intervention claims** — "Provided therapy" needs specifics; describe activities
- **Overlooking populations** — Experience breadth matters; list populations served
- **Generic rehab interest** — Show OTA-specific accomplishments

## Cover Letter Tips by Experience Level

### For New COTAs
- Highlight fieldwork and clinical education
- Show eagerness to develop skills under supervision
- Demonstrate understanding of OT/OTA relationship
- Emphasize patient interaction ability

### For Experienced COTAs
- Lead with patient outcomes and productivity
- Show population expertise and versatility
- Highlight specialized skills and certifications
- Include mentoring of students if applicable

### For Senior COTAs
- Emphasize student supervision and training
- Show program development contributions
- Highlight quality improvement involvement
- Demonstrate leadership within OTA role

## Adapting for Different Settings

**Skilled Nursing Facility:** Focus on ADL restoration, discharge planning, and regulatory compliance. Show understanding of SNF productivity and documentation requirements.

**Outpatient Clinic:** Emphasize home programs, patient education, and functional return. Show ability to motivate patients in ambulatory setting.

**Acute Care Hospital:** Highlight early mobility, safety assessment, and rapid evaluation. Show understanding of acute medical complexity.

**Pediatrics:** Focus on developmental activities, play-based intervention, and family involvement. Show child-appropriate therapeutic approaches.
