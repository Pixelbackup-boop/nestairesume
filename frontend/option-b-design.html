✨ Why Two-Step Entry Works
🎯 Reduces Cognitive Load Users make one decision at a time instead of facing 50+ options at once
👁️ Visual-First Approach See and compare templates before committing to one
🔀 Clear Builder Paths Form Builder vs Canvas Editor are clearly differentiated
🎨 Pre-Customization Try different colors and layouts before entering the editor
⬅️ Easy Navigation Back button allows users to reconsider their choices
📱 Mobile-Friendly Step-by-step works better on smaller screens
