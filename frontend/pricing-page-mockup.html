Frequently Asked Questions

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Yes! You can upgrade your plan at any time and the new features will be available immediately. When downgrading, changes take effect at the end of your billing cycle.

What happens when I reach my download limit?

For Free users, you can watch a rewarded ad to get additional downloads. For paid users, you can wait until your monthly limit resets or upgrade to a higher plan.

Is the Starter plan really one-time?

Yes! Pay $3 once and get 10 downloads with full template access. Perfect for those who just need a few CVs and don't want a subscription.

Do saved CVs expire?

No, your saved CVs never expire. However, the number of CVs you can save depends on your plan. You can always delete old CVs to make room for new ones.

What are Premium templates?

Premium templates are exclusive designer-crafted templates only available to Diamond subscribers. These include unique layouts and creative designs for standout resumes.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Absolutely! You can cancel anytime from your account settings. You'll continue to have access until the end of your billing period.
