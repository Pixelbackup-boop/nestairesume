# Zety.com - Competitor Analysis

## Basic Info
- **URL:** https://zety.com/resume-builder
- **Title:** Free AI Resume Builder: Easy, Fast & Smart Resume
- **Tagline:** 750+ Resume Templates

## Key Features Highlighted
1. 18 resume templates + 37 color schemes
2. AI-generated bullet points based on job title
3. ATS Resume Checker with scoring
4. Cover Letter Generator (bundled with subscription)
5. Multiple download formats (TXT free, PDF/Word paid)
6. Built-in resume scoring tool

## Trust Signals
- **7,500+ to 10,000+ reviews** on Trustpilot
- **3.4-4.0/5 rating** (varies by source)
- 71% 5-star reviews
- 14-day money-back guarantee

## Pricing Model
- **Free:** TXT download only
- **14-Day Access:** $1.95 (intro), auto-renews at $25.95/4 weeks
- **Annual Plan:** $5.95/month (best value)

## USPs
1. Extensive template library (750+)
2. AI bullet point generation by job title
3. ATS scoring built-in
4. Multiple color scheme options (37)
5. Cover letter included with subscription

## Content Strategy Observations
- Massive template library as hook (750+)
- ATS optimization messaging
- Low intro price ($1.95) for conversion
- Bundled cover letter for value perception
- Color customization as differentiator

## Keywords Targeted
- Free AI Resume Builder
- Resume Templates
- ATS Resume Checker
- Cover Letter Generator
- Professional Resume

## Pricing Issues Noted
- Users complain about pricing transparency
- Auto-renewal frustrations
- Customer service complaints
- Free tier very limited (TXT only)

## SEO Tactics
- Year in page titles (2025/2026)
- Template count in headlines (750+)
- ATS scoring as trust builder
- Review integration (Trustpilot)
