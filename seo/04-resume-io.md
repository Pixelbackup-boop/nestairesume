# Resume.io - Competitor Analysis

## Basic Info
- **URL:** https://resume.io/
- **Title:** Resume.io: #1 Free Online Resume Builder | 41+ Million Users
- **Meta:** Resume.io is the #1 free online resume builder. Our resume maker makes it easy for you to create a resume you can be proud of in minutes!

## Headlines (H1/H2)
- H1: "Get hired at top companies" (with company logos)
- H2s: "Everything you need to build the best resume", "Unique features that make the difference", "A wide range of beautifully designed templates", "Resume.io helps 2 people get hired every minute"

## Key Features Highlighted
1. Free Resume Builder
2. Cover Letter Builder
3. 40+ Resume Templates
4. Job Search Resources
5. CV Builder (international)
6. Mobile App

## Trust Signals
- **41+ Million Users** (in title - massive social proof)
- **"2 people get hired every minute"** (success metric)
- Company logos (Google, Amazon, Microsoft, etc.)
- "#1 Free Online Resume Builder" claim
- High user count reinforced throughout

## CTAs Used
- "Create my resume" (primary)
- "Get Started"
- "Build My Resume"

## Pricing Model
- Free tier available
- Premium subscription

## USPs
1. Massive user base (41M+ - biggest social proof)
2. Success rate metric (2 hires/minute)
3. Company placement proof (logos)
4. International support (CV Builder)
5. Mobile app availability

## Content Strategy Observations
- User numbers as PRIMARY trust signal (title + throughout)
- Company logos for aspirational marketing
- Success metrics ("2 people hired every minute")
- "#1" positioning claim
- Simple, benefit-focused messaging
- International focus (CV for non-US markets)
