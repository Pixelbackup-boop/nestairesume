## PART 5: TRUST SIGNALS RANKING

**Most effective trust signals (in order):**

1. **User Count** - "41M+ users" (Resume.io), "4.2M users" (Rezi)
2. **Review Ratings** - Trustpilot integration, star ratings
3. **Company Logos** - "Get hired at Google, Amazon, Microsoft"
4. **Success Metrics** - "2 people get hired every minute"
5. **Money-Back Guarantee** - Reduces risk perception
6. **Security Badges** - SSL, data protection mentions

### Recommended for AICB:
- Add user counter (even if starting small: "Join 10,000+ job seekers")
- Integrate Trustpilot early
- Collect success stories (name + company hired at)
- Add "Money-back guarantee" if offering paid tier

## PART 6: PRICING STRATEGY PATTERNS

### Competitor Pricing Comparison:

| Competitor | Free | Trial | Monthly | Annual |
|------------|------|-------|---------|--------|
| Resume.io | Yes (limited) | - | ~$15 | ~$45 |
| Rezi.ai | Yes | - | $29 | $129 |
| ResumeBuilder | TXT only | $2.95/14d | $7.95 | - |
| Zety | TXT only | $1.95 | $25.95/4wk | $5.95/mo |
| MyPerfectResume | TXT only | $2.95/14d | $23.95 | $7.80/mo |
| Resume-Now | TXT only | $1.45/14d | (auto-renew) | - |

### Key Patterns:
- **Free tier = TXT download only** (industry standard)
- **$1.45-$2.95 trial** (low barrier to conversion)
- **$7-$25/month** for full access
- **Auto-renewal** is standard but causes complaints

### Recommended for AICB:
1. Free tier: PDF download with watermark OR limited templates
2. Trial: $2.99 for 7-14 days
3. Monthly: $9.99/month
4. Annual: $59/year ($4.99/mo) - best value messaging
5. **Lifetime option: $99-149** (differentiator - few competitors offer this)
