# Resume-Now.com - Competitor Analysis

## Basic Info
- **URL:** https://www.resume-now.com/
- **Description:** Online resume builder with guided prompts and templates

## Key Features Highlighted
1. **30+ Resume Templates** (ATS optimized)
2. Pre-written content suggestions
3. AI-powered resume tips
4. AI Summary Generator
5. Cover Letter Builder
6. ATS Resume Checker
7. Interview Prep documents
8. Real-time preview
9. Applicant database (employers can view profile)

## Trust Signals
- **4.5/5 on Trustpilot** (13,000+ reviews)
- **4.3/5 on Sitejabber** (4,067 reviews)
- User-friendly reputation
- Design-forward templates

## Pricing Model
- **Free:** Text-only resume (basic AI features)
- **Limited Access:** $1.45 for 14 days
- **Full Access:** $1.85 for 14 days + applicant database
- Auto-renews at higher price after trial

## USPs
1. Very low trial pricing ($1.45-$1.85)
2. Creative/design-forward templates
3. Applicant database feature (unique)
4. Interview prep included
5. Real-time preview editing

## Content Strategy Observations
- Extremely low entry pricing ($1.45)
- Creative templates targeting younger users
- Interview prep as value-add
- Applicant database as unique hook
- Simple, minimal builder interface
